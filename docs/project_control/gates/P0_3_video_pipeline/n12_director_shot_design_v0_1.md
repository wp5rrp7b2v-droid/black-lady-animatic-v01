# N12｜Cross + Rigid Smile Detail｜Director Shot Design V0.1

Status: `PRODUCT OWNER APPROVED / BUNDLE DESIGN NEXT`

Date: 2026-09-27

## 1. Narrative function

N12 continues the same narration immediately after N11:

`脖子上挂着一个十字架，脸上的笑容有些说不出的僵硬。`

N11 has already established:

- Neil is around fifty;
- his skin is abnormally pale;
- he is standing quietly inside the entrance;
- the audience has begun to scrutinize him.

N12 is the next escalation:

`the audience notices two more specific details — the cross and the unnaturally rigid smile.`

This is still observation, not a supernatural reveal.

## 2. Relationship to adjacent shots

Previous N11:
`person-level abnormality / age + pallor / quiet scrutiny`

N12:
`specific disturbing details / cross + rigid smile`

Next A04:
`Ning Qiushui silently observes Neil`

Therefore N12 should sharpen unease but stop before overt horror.

It must leave A04 meaningful: the viewer should understand why Ning keeps watching him.

## 3. Shot scale

Target:
`medium close-up / head-and-upper-chest`

Required coverage:

- face clearly readable;
- mouth and cheek tension readable;
- upper chest included;
- cross visibly present in lower portion of frame;
- no waist;
- no hands;
- no lower torso.

Unlike N11, N12 intentionally allows slightly more upper-chest coverage because the cross is a required narrative detail.

The frame must still be person-led, not costume-led.

## 4. Camera

- near eye level;
- mild 3/4 angle;
- slightly closer / more frontal than an ordinary portrait, but not flat ID-photo symmetry;
- Neil remains inside the entrance, facing outward toward the arriving group;
- camera remains on the outside / threshold-facing side.

Spatial information is continuity support only.

Do not widen simply to prove the doorway.

## 5. Composition

Preferred visual hierarchy:

`Neil eyes / mouth → rigid smile → cross → pale face / formal clothing → soft interior background`

Neil's face remains the primary subject.

The cross should be visible enough to register without becoming a religious-symbol hero shot.

Recommended placement:

- Neil slightly off-center;
- cross in lower third / lower-center area;
- no perfect central-axis “portrait + crucifix” symmetry;
- doorway or door-edge may be minimally present but should be less visually important than in N11.

## 6. The smile — key performance lock

The smile is:

`closed-mouth / restrained / visibly rigid / socially polite but emotionally unconvincing`

Specific performance cues:

- mouth corners lift only slightly;
- lips remain closed;
- smile line feels held rather than naturally emerging;
- cheeks and mouth show tension;
- eyes do not fully participate in the smile;
- expression is controlled, not warm.

The audience should read:

`he is smiling because he is supposed to smile, not because he feels warmth.`

Do not show:

- teeth;
- broad grin;
- obvious sinister smirk;
- exaggerated villain expression;
- asymmetrical sneer;
- horror-face distortion.

## 7. Cross — key object lock

The cross must be visible and unmistakable, but not sensationalized.

Requirements:

- one simple metallic cross / cruciform pendant;
- naturally hanging from Neil's neck / chest;
- scale consistent with formal attire;
- small realistic metallic highlights are acceptable;
- it must read clearly enough for the narration.

Do not:

- enlarge it unnaturally;
- center it as the entire composition;
- make it glow;
- add supernatural light;
- make it ornate beyond continuity;
- turn it into a close-up insert;
- add extra rosary / chains / multiple religious objects.

The cross is one disturbing observed detail, not the subject of the whole shot.

## 8. Neil appearance

Preserve canonical Neil:

- approximately fifty;
- pale / low-blood-color skin;
- same face, hair and grooming;
- same formal black butler clothing;
- white shirt;
- restrained realism.

N12 must not undo N11's pallor.

Skin can remain pale and slightly cool, but not:

- corpse blue;
- chalk white;
- wax figure;
- vampire styling;
- heavy makeup.

## 9. Spatial continuity

Neil is still:

- inside the open castle entrance;
- facing outward;
- waiting for the guests.

No new physical action occurs.

Do not show:

- turn;
- step;
- greeting gesture;
- hands;
- movement deeper into the castle;
- return to the exterior platform.

The background remains a darker, subordinate castle interior.

## 10. Lighting

Lighting should support the contrast between pale skin and dark formal clothing.

Preferred:

- soft exterior daylight reaching Neil's face;
- darker interior behind;
- restrained warm practical lights may remain in deep background;
- small natural metallic reflection on the cross is acceptable.

Avoid:

- religious spotlight;
- halo effect;
- hard horror lighting;
- bright glowing cross;
- dramatic color cast.

## 11. Visual tone

Target feeling:

`quietly uncanny, socially correct, emotionally wrong`

Not:

- horror poster;
- vampire portrait;
- church iconography;
- villain reveal;
- gothic fashion portrait.

The exact Bundle reference set remains a separate approval step.

## 13. Hard negatives

N12 fails if it becomes any of the following:

- full-body / half-body doorway portrait;
- cross close-up insert;
- glowing religious symbol;
- broad smile / teeth;
- sinister villain grin;
- horror makeup;
- exterior-platform Neil;
- hands or waist visible;
- overt supernatural transformation;
- centered ceremonial “butler + cross” portrait;
- architecture more prominent than Neil.

## 14. Acceptance criteria

N12 passes director review only if:

1. Neil identity remains stable;
2. face and rigid smile are immediately readable;
3. smile is closed-mouth, small and emotionally stiff;
4. cross is clearly visible but secondary to the face;
5. Neil remains inside the entrance;
6. no new action is introduced;
7. pallor from N11 continuity is preserved;
8. no hands / waist / broad architectural framing;
9. the frame feels observational rather than theatrical;
10. the shot increases unease without exhausting the horror escalation.
