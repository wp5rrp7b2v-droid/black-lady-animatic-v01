## 1. APPROVED CONTINUITY CORRECTION

The original N24 concept pushed the group too far into the castle before A06.

That would create a spatial/timing contradiction because A06 immediately returns attention to the still-open entrance door and the spoken question about why it is not being closed.

Approved V0.2 correction:

`N23: GROUP FINISHES ENTERING / NEIL REMAINS AT OPEN DOOR`

→

`N24: NEIL HAS JUST LEFT THE DOORWAY AND STARTS LEADING THE GROUP / THE GROUP IS STILL IN THE ENTRANCE-ADJACENT INTERIOR TRANSITION ZONE`

→

`A06: ATTENTION CUTS BACK TO THE STILL-OPEN DOOR / QUESTION IS RAISED`

N24 therefore does NOT depict the group already deep inside the castle.

The deeper unknown-space transition remains reserved for N27.

`POV + ENVIRONMENT TRANSITION + START OF INWARD MOVEMENT`

## 5. NEIL BLOCKING

Neil has just left the doorway and moved only a short distance ahead.

Required:

- farther ahead than Ning / nearby guests;
- rear / rear-3Q preferred;
- clearly leading the direction of travel;
- no theatrical gesture;
- no speaking emphasis yet;
- no hero centrality.

Neil must not already be deep inside the castle.

`HE HAS STARTED TO LEAD THEM`

`HE HAS ALREADY BROUGHT THEM TO THE NEXT MAJOR SPACE`
