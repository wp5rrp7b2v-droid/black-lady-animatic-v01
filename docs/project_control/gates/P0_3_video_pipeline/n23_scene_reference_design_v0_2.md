# N23｜Scene Reference Design V0.2

Status: `SUPERSEDED BY N23 SCENE REFERENCE DESIGN V0.2.1 / HISTORICAL CAMERA DESIGN`

Date: 2026-10-02

Shot:

`N23｜Neil at the Open Door — Watches the Group Enter`

Supersedes:

`N23 Scene Reference Design V0.1`

Revision trigger:

N23 Candidate 01 proved that the V0.1 camera family — camera deeper inside the castle looking back toward the entrance — remained too visually similar to N22 and produced another face-readable interior ensemble.

Product Owner corrected the camera geometry to a structurally different side-crossing view.

---

## 1. NEW CAMERA GEOMETRY — HARD LOCK

The authoritative screen-space relationship is:

`LEFT = OPEN CASTLE DOOR + CONTROLLED EXTERIOR LIGHT`

`CROWD MOTION = LEFT → RIGHT`

`RIGHT = DEEPER CASTLE INTERIOR`

`NEIL = BESIDE THE DOOR / NEAR-FRONTAL TO CAMERA / EYES OR HEAD SLIGHTLY TURNED LEFT TOWARD ENTERING PEOPLE`

This is the primary N23 geometry.

The shot must not return to:

`CAMERA DEEPER INSIDE → LOOKING BACK AT PEOPLE COMING TOWARD THE LENS`

That V0.1 camera family is superseded.

---

## 2. FORMAT

Target:

`9:16 VERTICAL / 941 × 1672`

All composition choices must be made natively for the vertical frame.

---

## 3. CAMERA POSITION

Camera is inside the castle, near the threshold zone, positioned on the interior side opposite Neil enough to observe him and the entering flow broadside.

The camera is:

- side / side-oblique to the people's travel vector;
- mildly elevated;
- approximately `10–20°` downward visual angle;
- not top-down;
- not centered on the doorway;
- not several meters deeper inside looking back toward the entrance.

Camera shorthand:

`SIDE / SLIGHTLY ELEVATED / BROADSIDE THRESHOLD VIEW`

The camera should make the left-to-right crossing readable immediately.

---

## 4. DOOR + LIGHT POSITION

The open castle door is on the:

`LEFT SIDE OF FRAME`

Exterior daylight / brighter threshold light also originates from the:

`LEFT SIDE`

The light is only a spatial cue.

It must remain:

- controlled;
- partial;
- subordinate to the warm-dark interior.

Prohibited:

- white portal;
- blown doorway;
- giant bright opening;
- broad daylight reflection across the floor.

The left side should explain where the guests came from, not dominate the frame.

---

## 5. CROWD MOVEMENT — HARD LOCK

People enter from the left and move toward the right.

Screen vector:

`LEFT → RIGHT`

Narrative vector:

`OPEN DOOR → CASTLE INTERIOR`

They should pass across the frame rather than approach the camera.

Preferred body presentation:

- side profile;
- side-back;
- partial profile;
- partial occlusion;
- different stride phases.

Do not stage:

- frontal walking toward lens;
- all faces readable;
- single-file queue;
- synchronized walking;
- symmetrical group formation.

This left-to-right lateral crossing is the principal visual difference from N22.

---

## 6. NEIL POSITION + ORIENTATION

Neil stands immediately inside / beside the doorway.

Because the camera is now broadside to the crossing flow, Neil may be:

`NEAR-FRONTAL TO CAMERA`

Preferred body relation:

- torso approximately facing camera;
- slight 3/4 deviation is acceptable;
- head and/or eyes subtly turned toward frame-left;
- he is watching the guests enter from the door side.

This is not a hero portrait.

Neil must remain integrated into the scene:

- medium-wide scale;
- not centered as a formal portrait;
- may be partially crossed / interrupted by moving people;
- restrained posture;
- no theatrical gesture.

Neil must not:

- touch the door;
- close the door;
- walk away yet;
- stare directly into lens;
- dominate the frame as a solo character image.

The intended read is:

`NEIL FACES GENERALLY TOWARD CAMERA WHILE HIS ATTENTION IS ON PEOPLE ENTERING FROM FRAME-LEFT`

---

## 7. RECOMMENDED SCREEN LAYOUT

### Left zone

- open door / door jamb;
- controlled exterior light;
- first entering figures crossing threshold.

### Left-center / middle zone

- Neil beside the door;
- near-frontal / restrained;
- head or eyes angled left;
- not centered as hero.

### Mid-to-right zone

- guests moving left → right;
- overlapping figures;
- side / side-back bodies;
- increasing darkness / interior depth.

### Right zone

- deeper castle continuation;
- no full First Hall reveal;
- no bright destination point.

---

## 8. N22 DIFFERENCE TEST

N22 and N23 must differ structurally.

### N22

- interior observer view;
- stronger face readability;
- emphasis on identifying people within the group.

### N23 V0.2

- threshold-side broadside view;
- door and light anchored left;
- crowd crosses left → right;
- Neil is near-frontal beside the door;
- emphasis is the open-door fact and Neil watching people pass.

Automatic FAIL if:

`N23 LOOKS LIKE N22 WITH ONLY A SMALL CAMERA SHIFT`

or:

`THE CROWD AGAIN WALKS TOWARD CAMERA AS A FACE-READABLE ENSEMBLE`

---

## 9. GUANG YONG CONTINUITY

Guang Yong may remain a low-weight continuity cue.

If visible:

- he moves left → right with the group;
- body continues toward interior;
- head may turn slightly back toward frame-left / Neil / door;
- side or side-back orientation preferred;
- partial occlusion is acceptable.

He must not become:

- first-glance subject;
- foreground portrait;
- equal co-lead with Neil;
- clearly speaking.

Hierarchy:

`NEIL + OPEN DOOR > LEFT-TO-RIGHT GROUP FLOW > GUANG YONG BACKWARD ATTENTION`

---

## 10. CROWD WEIGHT

Preferred:

`4–6 VISIBLE PEOPLE OR PARTIAL BODIES`

But named faces should not dominate.

Candidate 01 failure to avoid:

- Su / Liao / Guang forming another readable trio;
- several named characters simultaneously looking toward Neil;
- crowd becoming a reaction tableau.

N23 is about passage through the doorway, not character presentation.

---

## 11. LIGHT / COLOR

Maintain:

- warm amber / tungsten practical family;
- low-key interior;
- restrained saturation;
- natural skin;
- readable dark clothing / hair / stone;
- moderate cinematic contrast.

Candidate 01 failures explicitly prohibited:

- excessive exterior brightness;
- bright reflective floor;
- strong white outdoor area;
- lighting that pulls attention away from Neil + open-door state.

---

## 12. ACCESSORIES

Hard prohibition:

- backpacks;
- shoulder bags;
- handbags;
- luggage;
- travel bags;
- visible straps implying carried baggage.

Candidate 01's ambiguous bag-like foreground element must not recur.
