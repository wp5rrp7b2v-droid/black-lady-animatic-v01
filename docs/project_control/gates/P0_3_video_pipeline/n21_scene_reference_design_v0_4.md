## 1. Revision purpose

Candidate 01–03 repeatedly exposed a tradeoff between:

- crowd scale;
- atmosphere;
- spatial logic;
- Ning Qiushui / Jun Luyuan identity;
- body posture;
- natural crowd behavior.

V0.4 therefore does not attempt to fix Candidate 03 by adding more pose/crowd/prompt constraints.

Core principle:

`SIMPLIFY STORY FUNCTION / DO NOT RELAX CHARACTER VISUAL STYLE`

中文定义：

`简化叙事职责 / 不放松人物视觉风格`

## 2. N21 primary narrative function

N21 now primarily owns:

`SHOW A GROUP OF PARTICIPANTS CROSSING FROM THE CASTLE ENTRANCE INTO A DARKER, UNKNOWN AND POTENTIALLY DANGEROUS INTERIOR`

第一眼应读成：

`他们正在走进一个未知的地方`

而不是：

- `这里完整展示了16名参与者`
- `这里重点展示宁秋水和君鹭远`

N21 therefore changes from:

`COHORT SCALE PROOF`

to:

`THRESHOLD TRANSITION + UNKNOWN-SPACE MOOD`

## 3. Sixteen-person story fact

Canonical fact remains:

`16 PARTICIPANTS TOTAL`

But:

`16 PEOPLE ≠ SINGLE-FRAME COUNTING REQUIREMENT`

V0.4 does not require:

- sixteen visible full bodies;
- sixteen visible heads;
- sixteen countable participants;
- the 8/8 gender split to be visually proven in this frame.

Preferred visible complexity:

`APPROXIMATELY 4–6 READABLE HUMAN FIGURES`

Additional participants may be implied through:

- foreground cropping;
- partial bodies;
- occlusion;
- silhouettes;
- deeper-space figures;
- off-frame continuation.

The image must not contradict the sixteen-person fact, but does not need to prove it numerically.

## 4. Ning Qiushui / Jun Luyuan responsibility

Ning Qiushui and Jun Luyuan are no longer mandatory identity targets for N21.

They may:

- appear partially;
- appear rear-facing;
- be partly occluded;
- occupy secondary visual positions;
- be visually integrated into the group.

They must not be deliberately staged into:

- a hero two-shot;
- a central poster composition;
- a clean paired silhouette;
- a camera-facing presentation.

If either character is sufficiently visible for identity judgment, identity must remain compatible with the approved character system.

However:

`N21 DOES NOT NEED TO PROVE THEIR IDENTITY`

Clearer identity continuity may resume in later Story Shots.

## 5. Character Visual Style Lock

This is a hard requirement.

Even though named-character identity burden is reduced:

`CHARACTER VISUAL STYLE CONTINUITY IS NOT REDUCED`

All visible people must remain inside the established visual language of the approved Story Shot series.

Required continuity includes:

### 5.1 Face construction

Maintain:

- realistic human facial proportions;
- restrained cinematic facial detail;
- the same live-action realism as approved Story Shots.

Avoid:

- excessive beauty-retouch look;
- commercial-advertising facial treatment;
- fashion-editorial treatment;
- game-NPC facial language;
- anime / illustration drift;
- generic synthetic crowd-face appearance.

### 5.2 Age and physical presence

Maintain believable adult age variation.

Avoid:

- everyone appearing the same age;
- excessive youthification;
- beauty-model homogenization;
- repeated facial structures.

### 5.3 Skin rendering

Maintain:

- natural skin texture;
- realistic low-key lighting response;
- restrained highlights.

Avoid:

- plastic skin;
- excessive smoothing;
- glossy commercial skin;
- orange skin cast.

### 5.4 Hair

Hair must remain:

- physically believable;
- naturally separated from face and clothing;
- compatible with existing Story Shot realism.

Avoid:

- synthetic wig-like volume;
- fashion-editorial styling;
- repeated identical hairstyles.

### 5.5 Body proportions

Maintain:

- realistic adult proportions;
- believable shoulder / spine / hip alignment;
- natural standing and walking balance.

Avoid:

- accidental hunched silhouettes;
- malformed torso proportions;
- elongated limbs;
- stylized fashion-model proportions.

### 5.6 Clothing rendering

Maintain:

- contemporary believable clothing;
- realistic fabric weight;
- restrained styling;
- natural folds;
- existing project material language.

Avoid:

- sudden costume redesign;
- fashion-campaign presentation.

## 9. Composition freedom

V0.4 deliberately does not lock:

- exact shot size;
- camera height;
- exact camera direction;
- exact number of visible complete bodies;
- foreground character identity;
- exact character positions;
- exact walking phase.

Director composition should serve one question:

`DOES THE VIEWER FEEL THAT PEOPLE ARE CROSSING INTO AN UNKNOWN SPACE?`

Preferred cinematic devices may include:

- architectural framing;
- foreground crop;
- partial silhouettes;
- depth layering;
- occlusion;
- uneven spacing;
- darker destination space;
- locally visible warm practical light.

## 10. Crowd movement

Movement remains:

`ENTRANCE → DEEPER INTERIOR`

V0.4 removes the requirement to visually demonstrate the entire cohort transition.

Visible figures should show:

`ONE GENERAL DIRECTION / NATURAL INDIVIDUAL VARIATION`

Avoid:

- orderly queue;
- synchronized walking;
- repeated identical stride;
- everyone facing the same angle;
- mass head-turning;
- group-photo spacing;
- commercial ensemble staging.

The crowd should feel accidentally observed during movement rather than posed for the camera.

## 11. Space and threat atmosphere

N21 should communicate:

`KNOWN ENTRANCE → LESS-KNOWN INTERIOR`

Unknown-space feeling should come primarily from:

- reduced visibility deeper inside;
- architectural compression;
- partial obstruction;
- diminishing practical light;
- darkness with readable texture;
- incomplete spatial information.

Do not create threat through:

- unsupported supernatural visual effects;
- fog-heavy fantasy treatment;
- horror-game lighting;
- exaggerated gothic spectacle.

Danger should feel latent rather than explicitly supernatural.

## 12. Color / visual-world continuity

N21 remains part of the same continuous castle sequence.

Required:

- warm low-key practical-light family;
- natural skin tones;
- restrained saturation;
- moderate cinematic contrast;
- readable dark textures;
- broadly continuous principal light logic.

Allowed:

- the deeper interior may naturally become darker than N20.

Not allowed:

- orange-heavy global cast;
- gray desaturation reset;
- blue horror-film reset;
- over-bright hall;
- crushed black silhouettes with no readable human material;
- commercial glossy lighting.

## 13. Character Style Hard Gate

A candidate automatically fails if any major visible person shows clear visual-language drift such as:

1. facial-generation style clearly different from approved Story Shots;
2. commercial / fashion-model rendering;
3. plastic or over-smoothed skin;
4. game-character / NPC appearance;
5. stylized or exaggerated human proportions;
6. clothing rendered in a materially different visual language;
7. obvious age / facial homogenization across the crowd;
8. malformed or implausible body posture;
9. characters appearing to belong to a different visual work.

This gate applies even if:

- atmosphere is excellent;
- architecture is correct;
- composition is strong;
- crowd movement is otherwise successful.

`CHARACTER STYLE DRIFT = DIRECTOR FAIL`

## 14. V0.4 acceptance test

A future N21 candidate passes only if all are true:

1. first read = people are entering a darker unknown castle interior;
2. threshold / transition feeling is clear;
3. visible people remain part of the established character visual system;
4. no major human-style drift;
5. crowd behavior feels naturally observed rather than staged;
6. approximately 4–6 readable figures are sufficient;
7. exact sixteen-person count is not required;
8. Ning / Jun do not dominate;
9. named-character presentation is not attempted;
10. scene remains entrance-to-interior transition space;
11. no full First Hall reveal;
12. atmosphere is more unknown / threatening than ordinary pedestrian movement;
13. lighting and material world remain compatible with N20 and the approved Story Shot sequence.
