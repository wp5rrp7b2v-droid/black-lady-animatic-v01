`EXPAND THE VISUAL WORLD FROM N20'S TWO-PERSON BEAT TO THE FULL SIXTEEN-PERSON COHORT ENTERING THE CASTLE`

## 3. Composition policy

A wide / wider group-scale interior view is preferred because N21 must create an immediate scale change from N20.

However, exact:

- shot size;
- camera height;
- camera direction;
- foreground placement;
- occlusion;
- character position;
- body pose;

remain Director decisions at generation / candidate review level.

The approved requirement is the function, not one frozen composition template.

The image should make the cohort feel like it is actively filtering from the entrance into the castle rather than standing for a group photograph.

## 4. Crowd Complexity Control

The story fact is:

`16 PARTICIPANTS / MEN AND WOMEN EVENLY SPLIT`

This does NOT require sixteen equally clear faces or sixteen fully visible bodies.

Preferred stability strategy:

- approximately 4–6 figures in comparatively readable zones;
- remaining figures may be rear-facing, side-facing, partially cropped, partially occluded, smaller, darker or more distant;
- avoid requiring all hands, feet and faces to be simultaneously clear;
- clear frontal faces should remain limited where possible;
- natural overlap is desirable if it improves crowd realism and reduces artificial lineup staging.

If every person is fully countable, the count must equal 16.

If natural occlusion prevents exact counting, the frame must still plausibly read as the complete sixteen-person cohort.

Do not visually reduce the group to only 6–8 people.

Do not inflate it into a much larger crowd.

## 5. Crowd Motion & Staging Rule

Core rule:

`ONE GLOBAL MOVEMENT DIRECTION / LOCAL ASYNCHRONY`

Primary movement:

`CASTLE ENTRANCE → INTERIOR`

The crowd must not behave as sixteen synchronized performers.

Preferred action language:

- some participants just crossing the threshold;
- some already moving farther inside;
- some following companions;
- some slightly adjusting body direction or spacing;
- different gait phases;
- natural overlap.

Action hierarchy:

### Primary actions

Only 1–2 clearly readable action moments are needed.

Examples may include:

- crossing the threshold;
- slight sidestep / spatial adjustment.

### Secondary actions

A small number of figures may carry:

- varied gait phase;
- mild head turn;
- companion-following behavior;
- minor speed difference.

### Background / tertiary actions

Most other figures only need to support believable group flow.

Avoid:

- synchronized walking poses;
- repeated identical strides;
- multiple strong hand gestures;
- widespread head-turning;
- complex multi-person physical interaction;
- orderly formation;
- class-photo / publicity-photo staging.

Even if anatomy and identity technically pass, a crowd that feels posed, cloned or mechanically synchronized is a Director FAIL.

## 10. Color / look continuity

N21 must clearly belong to the same visual world as N20.

Baseline:

- warm low-key castle interior;
- natural skin tone;
- restrained saturation;
- readable dark texture;
- moderate cinematic contrast;
- continuous principal light logic.

but brightness must remain localized near the doorway.

- neutral-gray visual reset;
- over-bright interior;
- exterior light flooding the whole floor;
