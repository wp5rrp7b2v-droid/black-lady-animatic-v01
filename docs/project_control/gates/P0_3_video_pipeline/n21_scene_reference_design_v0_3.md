## 3. Crowd complexity rule

Preferred stability strategy:

- approximately 4–6 comparatively readable figures;
- remaining figures may be rear-facing, side-facing, smaller, partially cropped, partially occluded or deeper in shadow;
- avoid requiring all faces, hands and feet to be simultaneously clear;
- clear frontal faces should remain limited;
- natural overlap is encouraged where it improves realism and lowers AI crowd-failure risk.

## 4. Crowd motion rule

Locked movement logic:

`ONE GLOBAL MOVEMENT DIRECTION / LOCAL ASYNCHRONY`

Global movement:

`CASTLE ENTRANCE → INTERIOR`

The cohort should feel like a real flow of people:

- varied gait phases;
- natural spacing;
- small companion groupings;
- mild local direction differences;
- some already inside / some crossing / some still nearer the entrance.

Avoid:

- synchronized walking;
- repeated identical strides;
- lineup staging;
- publicity-photo staging;
- mass camera-facing poses;
- widespread strong hand gestures;
- complex multi-person physical interactions.

Crowd action quality is a Director gate. A technically clean but posed / cloned crowd is a FAIL.

## 5. Ning / Jun continuity

Ning and Jun may remain recognizable but must not dominate.

They must:

- continue moving inward;
- preserve N20 wardrobe / appearance continuity;
- avoid a clean hero two-shot;
- avoid camera-facing pose;
- avoid resumed private-conversation staging.

N20 remains sufficient first-pass appearance authority for both characters.

Separate Ning / Jun Character Reference Sheets are not required in V0.3.

## 6. Space boundary

Scene remains:

`CASTLE_ENTRANCE → INTERIOR TRANSITION ZONE`

Allowed:

- open main doors;
- entrance stone architecture;
- arches / pillars;
- limited interior depth;
- warm practical lighting.

Do not reveal:

- full First Hall;
- A07-style hall establishment;
- mural corridor;
- unrelated castle architecture.

N21 expands people scale, not building scale.

## 7. Color / look continuity

N21 must belong to the same visual world as N20.

Generation-stage continuity:

- warm low-key interior;
- natural skin;
- restrained low-to-moderate saturation;
- readable dark texture;
- moderate cinematic contrast;
- broadly continuous light direction.

Entrance may be somewhat brighter / cooler, but brighter exterior light must remain localized near the doorway.

Avoid:

- global orange cast;
- neutral-gray reset;
- over-bright interior;
- hard daylight flooding the whole foreground;
- crushed featureless blacks.
