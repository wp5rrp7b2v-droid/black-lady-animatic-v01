## 1. Product Owner decision

N26 and N27 are merged into one Story Shot.

N26 now owns both functions:

1. rule-source completion — `这是夫人的要求。`
2. forward transition / invitation — `各位，请随我来。`

N27 is no longer a separate production node.

## 2. Narrative function

N26 is:

`EXPLANATION CLOSE + LEADERSHIP RESUMES + GROUP FORWARD MOTION`

It ends the entrance-door exchange and shifts the cohort back into movement toward the deeper castle interior.

## 3. Character hierarchy

### First subject

`NEIL`

Neil remains the narrative leader and visual first subject.

### Key secondary characters

`NING QIUSHUI + JUN LUYUAN`

Both are intentionally reintroduced in N26.

They should be:

- clearly recognizable;
- readable within the moving group;
- following Neil;
- secondary to Neil rather than equal hero subjects.

### Tertiary layer

Anonymous / generic guests support the larger cohort.

## 4. Continuity from N25

N25 established:

- Neil walking among the group;
- Neil speaking without turning back;
- the group beginning to orient toward him;
- Castle Entrance Inner Lobby continuity;
- no visible main door;
- no First Hall reveal.

N26 must advance, not repeat.

Locked progression:

`N25 = NEIL MOVES / GROUP MOSTLY PAUSED`

`N26 = NEIL LEADS / GROUP BEGINS FOLLOWING`

## 5. First-read hierarchy

First read:

`NEIL IS NOW LEADING THE GROUP FORWARD`

Second read:

`NING QIUSHUI AND JUN LUYUAN ARE CLEARLY BACK IN THE FOLLOWING COHORT`

Third read:

`THE REST OF THE GUESTS BEGIN FLOWING AFTER THEM`

Do not make first read:

- Ning + Jun dual portrait;
- three-person hero poster;
- Neil stopping to address the crowd;
- full next-room reveal.

## 6. Neil performance

Dialogue:

`这是夫人的要求。各位，请随我来。`

Performance:

`CALM / ROUTINE / MATTER-OF-FACT / LIGHTLY DETACHED`

Neil:

- continues moving;
- leads toward deeper interior;
- may show side / side-back / rear-3Q;
- may have small natural speaking mouth movement;
- does not need a large turn-back;
- does not stop for a formal address;
- no theatrical gesture required.

## 7. Ning Qiushui

Ning is a key secondary follower.

Required:

- recognizable identity;
- integrated naturally into the moving cohort;
- side / 3Q / side-back acceptable;
- no direct camera stare;
- no posed hero stance;
- hands remain natural.

Hard rule:

`NING QIUSHUI MUST NOT PUT HANDS IN POCKETS`

## 8. Jun Luyuan

Jun is a key secondary follower.

Required:

- recognizable identity;
- visible enough to re-establish main-character continuity;
- side / 3Q / side-back acceptable;
- naturally following;
- no direct camera stare;
- no posed portrait stance.

## 9. Ning + Jun relationship

They may appear in the same shot.

But forbid:

- frontal two-person portrait;
- shoulder-to-shoulder paired pose;
- equal symmetrical placement;
- matching gaze;
- poster-like couple / duo composition.

Preferred:

`NATURAL STAGGER / DIFFERENT DEPTH / DIFFERENT SCREEN POSITION`

## 10. Group motion

N26 is the first beat in this micro-sequence where group motion becomes clearly active.

Required:

- several guests begin following;
- varied stride;
- varied orientation;
- imperfect spacing;
- natural occlusion;
- no single-file queue;
- no synchronized marching.

Neil initiates movement.

Ning and Jun are among the more readable followers.

Generic guests complete the flow.

## 11. Camera / composition

Target:

`9:16 VERTICAL`

Preferred:

`MEDIUM-WIDE / GROUP TRANSITION SHOT`

Camera may sit:

- slightly front-side of the moving cohort;
- oblique to the movement axis;
- or lateral enough to read Neil plus Ning / Jun without flattening everyone into a line.

The shot should feel like the cohort is beginning to pass through the frame toward deeper interior.

## 12. Space

Remain in:

`CASTLE_ENTRANCE_INNER_LOBBY / EXITING TOWARD DEEPER CASTLE`

Allowed:

- stronger inward directional depth than N25;
- arch / passage cues;
- transition-space feeling.

Not allowed:

- full First Hall establishment;
- fireplace;
- dining setup;
- grand staircase;
- major next-room reveal;
- visible exterior.

## 13. Door handling

The open-door issue is finished.

N26 must not return attention to the entrance.

No:

- main door as focal object;
- exterior opening;
- rain;
- door closing action.

## 14. Lighting

Continue:

- warm low-key;
- restrained amber / tungsten;
- natural skin;
- textured blacks;
- dark stone / wood;
- restrained saturation;
- clarity without raising overall brightness.

All characters must share the same environmental light.

No cutout / pasted-on layers.

## 15. Hard fail

Automatic FAIL if:

- Neil stops and turns fully to give a speech;
- Ning and Jun become a frontal paired portrait;
- Neil + Ning + Jun form a three-person poster;
- Ning puts hands in pockets;
- group becomes a queue;
- synchronized walking dominates;
- visible main door returns;
- rain appears;
- First Hall is fully revealed;
- fireplace appears;
- bags / luggage appear;
- studio-bright lighting;
- cutout / compositing look;
- identity drift of Neil / Ning / Jun.

## 16. Minimum proof

PASS if:

1. Neil is first subject and clearly leading;
2. the group has begun following;
3. Ning Qiushui is recognizable and naturally integrated;
4. Jun Luyuan is recognizable and naturally integrated;
5. Ning and Jun are staggered rather than posed together;
6. Ning does not put hands in pockets;
7. motion is natural and unsynchronized;
8. scene remains entrance-inner-lobby transition;
9. no door / rain / First Hall reveal;
10. the shot clearly advances beyond N25.
