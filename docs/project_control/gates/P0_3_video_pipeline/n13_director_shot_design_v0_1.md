# N13｜Waist Suspicion Bridge｜Director Shot Design V0.1

Status: `PRODUCT OWNER APPROVED / BUNDLE DESIGN AUTHORIZED`

Date: 2026-09-27

## 1. Narrative function

N13 shifts audience attention away from Neil's face and toward his waist area without revealing the answer.

Sequence logic:

`N11 abnormal person → N12 cross + rigid smile → A04 Ning observes → N13 gaze moves downward → N15 suspense tightens → A05 reveal: waist empty / no keys`

N13 must create the feeling that Ning Qiushui is checking another important detail, while preserving A05 as the exclusive reveal.

## 2. Shot type

Target:
`torso transition shot / lower medium close-up`

Visual range:

- upper frame begins around lower face / neck / bow-tie area;
- lower frame approaches the waist but does not fully expose the answer zone;
- no full-body framing;
- no clear "empty waist" read.

## 3. Composition

- 9:16 vertical;
- Neil remains the subject;
- visual weight moves from upper torso toward vest / lower jacket / near-waist region;
- camera logic continues from outside / threshold-facing side toward Neil inside;
- architecture remains subordinate.

The viewer should understand:
`we are now checking lower on Neil's body`

but should not yet understand:
`there are no keys`.

## 4. Performance and action

Neil remains:

- inside the entrance;
- facing outward;
- still;
- not speaking;
- not gesturing.

Do not introduce:

- reaching for pockets;
- touching clothing;
- drawing attention to waist with hands;
- turning or stepping;
- any action that functions as a reveal.

## 5. Prop / continuity boundaries

- Cross is not a narrative focus in N13.
- Chain may be present or partly obscured, but should not attract attention.
- Keys must not appear.
- More importantly, the absence of keys must not yet be visually explicit.
- A05 exclusively owns the reveal that Neil's waist is empty.

## 6. Hard negatives

Fail if:

- waist is fully exposed and obviously empty;
- shot duplicates A05;
- keys appear;
- hands point toward or touch the waist;
- image becomes a clothing catalog portrait;
- face/cross becomes the primary subject again;
- wide doorway / full-body architecture dominates.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

N13 passes if:

1. attention clearly moves downward from previous face-focused shots;
2. torso / vest / near-waist region becomes the new visual interest;
3. Neil identity and inside-threshold continuity remain stable;
4. no new action is introduced;
5. "no keys" is still unresolved;
6. the shot naturally hands off to N15 and then A05.

Director design is approved.
