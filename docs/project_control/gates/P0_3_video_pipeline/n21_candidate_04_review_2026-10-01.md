## 6. Reference-strategy lesson

A complete approved Story Shot is not a sufficiently isolated style carrier for this task.

N03 can transmit not only:

- human realism;
- face / skin / hair rendering;
- clothing material language;

but also:

- concrete wardrobe combinations;
- figure grouping;
- shot-specific population cues.

Therefore N03 must not be reused as the formal character-style generation input for the next N21 attempt.
