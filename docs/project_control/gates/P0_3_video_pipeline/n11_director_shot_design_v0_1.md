# N11｜Neil Abnormal Portrait｜Director Shot Design V0.1

Status: `PRODUCT OWNER APPROVED / SPATIAL CONTINUITY CORRECTED / BUNDLE DESIGN NEXT`

Approved on: 2026-09-27

## 1. Narrative function

N11 begins the post-Opening scrutiny of Neil.

It corresponds to the narration that Neil is extremely pale and appears to be around fifty years old.

The shot shifts the audience from “formal welcoming butler” to “a person Ning Qiushui is now carefully evaluating.”

## 2. Corrected spatial continuity

Product Owner correction:

- Neil has already crossed the castle threshold;
- he is now standing on the **interior side of the open entrance**;
- he faces outward toward the ghost guests who are still outside / entering;
- he is waiting for them rather than continuing the welcome action;
- he is not outside the castle and not straddling the threshold.

This spatial condition is now locked for N11.

## 3. Composition

- target: 9:16 vertical Story Shot;
- chest-up / medium close-up;
- Neil is the only clear subject;
- camera positioned near the exterior/threshold side, looking slightly inward toward Neil;
- eye-level or very slightly lower than eye level;
- near-frontal with a mild 3/4 turn, not a flat ID-photo front;
- Neil is clearly **inside** the door;
- open door frame / threshold geometry remains legible enough to prove spatial continuity;
- interior behind Neil is darker than the exterior approach, but not crushed into black;
- outside visitors may be implied off-camera and must not dominate the image.

Preferred visual hierarchy:

`Neil face → abnormal pallor / age → formal butler clothing → interior-side open-door context`

## 4. Performance

Neil:

- upright and formal;
- still, controlled, waiting;
- mouth closed;
- no active speaking pose;
- neutral-to-faintly-stiff expression;
- no friendly smile;
- no exaggerated horror grin;
- no turn, step, or hand gesture as a narrative action.

The unease comes from pallor, stillness, and over-controlled demeanor rather than monsterization.

## 5. Appearance continuity

Must preserve canonical Neil identity:

- same face and age structure;
- same hair / grooming;
- same formal black butler suit and white shirt;
- skin clearly pale but not corpse-blue, chalk-white, blown-out, or theatrical makeup;
- approximate fifty-year-old appearance readable.

The cross may be incidentally visible, but it must not become the focal point in N11. N12 owns the cross + rigid-smile emphasis.

## 6. Lighting / tone

- preserve castle entrance day/open-door continuity;
- outside light may shape Neil from the front/side while the interior behind him remains dimmer;
- facial structure and age detail must remain visible;
- restrained uncanny mood;
- no horror-poster lighting.

Continuity references:

Continuity Story Shots may guide spatial/costume continuity only and may not override canonical character or Scene Master authority.

## 8. Hard negatives

Do not show:

- Neil outside the castle;
- Neil straddling the threshold;
- active welcome/speaking gesture;
- open mouth;
- broad smile;
- exaggerated rigid grin reserved for N12;
- walking / turning;
- waist inspection or keys;
- overt supernatural transformation;
- visitors dominating foreground;
- Dutch angle / horror-poster staging.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

N11 passes director review only if:

1. Neil identity is stable;
2. Neil is unmistakably on the interior side of the open door;
3. he faces outward / toward the arriving group;
4. pallor and approximate age read immediately;
5. the image feels observational, not like another welcome shot;
6. N12 still has room to escalate;
7. no waist/key information is leaked.
