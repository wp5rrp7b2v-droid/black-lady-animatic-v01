## 1. Bundle objective

V007 is the first Bundle for the fully redesigned N21.

It intentionally abandons the earlier N21 production architecture built around:

- complete threshold environment;
- architecture-first staging;
- broad corridor / portal continuity;
- dedicated four-person body / wardrobe reference.

The new test isolates only two visual facts:

1. `THIS IS THE SAME OPEN CASTLE DOOR`
2. `THESE ARE PROJECT-CONSISTENT ANONYMOUS GUESTS`

Everything else is controlled by written Story Shot authority.

Primary learning objective:

`CAN WORK CREATE A NATURAL, EMBEDDED THRESHOLD-CROWD FLOW SHOT WITHOUT QUEUE BIAS OR ARCHITECTURE SHOWCASE WHEN ONLY DOOR IDENTITY + REAR-3Q GUEST IDENTITY ARE VISUALLY DELIVERED?`

## 4. Story function

N21 first read:

`THE TWO-PERSON MOVEMENT HAS OPENED INTO A LARGER FLOW OF PEOPLE CROSSING THE SAME ENTRANCE THRESHOLD`

Primary function:

`SCALE SHIFT + THRESHOLD FLOW`

Secondary:

`PARTIAL SPATIAL UNCERTAINTY`

Threat:

`LATENT ONLY`

N21 is not:

- a sixteen-person proof frame;
- a group portrait;
- a named-character presentation shot;
- a Neil shot;
- a full entrance establishing shot;
- a Gothic hall showcase;
- a horror-effect tableau.

## 5. Camera authority — written only

Camera:

`INSIDE CASTLE / JUST BEYOND THRESHOLD / SHOULDER HEIGHT / OFF-AXIS / LOOKING DIAGONALLY INWARD`

Camera relation:

`AMONG / BESIDE THE MOVING PEOPLE`

not:

`STANDING BACK TO PHOTOGRAPH THE GROUP`

Spatial read:

- same open entrance remains behind / beside the moving flow;
- partial physical right-side carved door leaf appears only as a peripheral continuity cue;
- preferred final screen location = extreme screen-left;
- exterior daylight appears only as a narrow sliver beside the door;
- people move from left-rear / threshold toward right-forward / deeper interior;
- camera stays close enough for natural body cropping and overlap;
- no centered movement axis;
- no centered architectural vanishing point.

The written shot authority overrides any direction or framing implied by either reference image.

## 6. Human execution target

Total visible human presence:

`APPROXIMATELY 4–6 FIGURES / FIGURE FRAGMENTS`

Only:

`3–4 NEED COHERENT READABLE FULL / NEAR-FULL BODY SILHOUETTES`

The remaining human presence may be:

- edge-cropped;
- partially occluded;
- softer;
- farther inside.

Use anonymous identities selected from Guest A–J.

Do not force all ten.

Do not attempt to visually prove all sixteen participants.

Prefer a mixed male / female presence without turning the frame into an exact sex-count proof.

Desired body-language mixture:

- one cropped near body;
- one side-back / rear-3Q body;
- one or two midground rear-3Q figures;
- one deeper partially hidden figure.

Required:

- unequal lateral positions;
- unequal depths;
- unequal gaps;
- asynchronous stride phases;
- natural overlap;
- slight local facing variation;
- one shared general inward flow.

Forbidden:

- single-file queue;
- V formation;
- front-two / middle-two / rear-one formation;
- equal spacing;
- synchronized gait;
- central leader;
- everyone fully separated and readable;
- exact recreation of the 2×5 board.
