## 1. Source-grounded story facts

At this point in the original story:

- there are sixteen participants at the castle;
- the observed group is described as men and women evenly split;
- Jun Luyuan stands beside Ning Qiushui;
- after Jun acknowledges Ning's reminder, the participants find their teammates and enter the castle in sequence;
- the narrative does NOT formally introduce Su Xiaoxiao, Liao Jian, Wen Qingya or Guang Yong at this exact moment.

Later source facts establish:

### Su Xiaoxiao

- already belongs to the participant cohort;
- later naturally interacts with Liao Jian;
- visually presents as a beautiful young woman at this stage;
- the later reveal about Su Xiaoxiao's biological sex must NOT be foreshadowed visually here.

### Liao Jian

- later appears as a young man who exercises regularly;
- has an established familiar / teasing interaction dynamic with Su Xiaoxiao.

### Wen Qingya

- later formally appears wearing round-frame glasses;
- has a literary / cultured visual impression;
- has prior Blood Door experience and is bringing a friend through this door.

### Guang Yong

- later formally appears as short and slightly chubby;
- becomes memorable to Ning because he asks Neil an important question.

These later facts may guide character seeding, but N21 must not pretend the original story has formally introduced these characters already.

## 4. Character blocking

### Layer A｜Near-mid continuity layer

`NING QIUSHUI + JUN LUYUAN`

Purpose:

- preserve direct continuity from N20;
- show that the private two-person beat has now been absorbed into the larger group.

Rules:

- both still moving inward;
- identifiable but no longer dominant;
- do not isolate them in a clean hero two-shot;
- no shoulder contact;
- no resumed private conversation emphasis.

### Layer B｜Mid-layer seed pair

`SU XIAOXIAO + LIAO JIAN`

Preferred placement:

- same loose sub-cluster;
- walking near each other naturally;
- not posing;
- no exaggerated comic interaction yet.

Su Xiaoxiao visual lock:

- read as an attractive, petite / relatively small-framed young woman;
- feminine styling consistent with approved Character Reference;
- do NOT visually reveal or hint at the later gender reveal;
- do NOT make the character masculine as a clue.

Liao Jian visual lock:

- young adult man;
- physically fit / regularly exercises;
- grounded contemporary clothing;
- no exaggerated bodybuilder silhouette.

Their proximity is a continuity seed for their later familiar interaction, not a formal relationship announcement.

### Layer C｜Mid-side seed

`WEN QINGYA`

Preferred placement:

- slightly separated from the Ning / Jun and Su / Liao clusters;
- may be walking with one unnamed companion;
- round-frame glasses should remain readable if composition allows;
- quiet, observant presence;
- cultured / literary visual impression;
- no direct interaction with Ning yet.

Do NOT stage her as already confronting or analyzing Ning.

### Layer D｜Rear-mid seed

`GUANG YONG`

Preferred placement:

- within another small participant cluster;
- short / slightly chubby silhouette;
- readable as a distinct person but not highlighted;
- not yet asking Neil a question;
- no special gesture.

This preserves the later story logic that Ning remembers him because of a subsequent interaction rather than because N21 already featured him prominently.

## 5. Remaining ten participants

The other ten people remain unnamed cohort members.

Requirements:

- visually distinct adult individuals;
- varied hairstyles / body types / restrained contemporary clothing;
- no duplicated faces;
- no repeated identical outfits;
- natural teammate clustering;
- no need for equal visibility.

Their function is to make the sixteen-person cohort feel complete and believable.

## 6. Sixteen-person / gender-balance rule

Source visual fact:

`16 PEOPLE / MEN AND WOMEN APPEAR EVENLY SPLIT`

For screen design, interpret this as:

`8 MALE-PRESENTING + 8 FEMALE-PRESENTING AT THIS MOMENT`

Important:

Su Xiaoxiao must visually count on the female-presenting side in N21 because the later reveal has not occurred and must not be spoiled.

Hard rules:

- if all figures are clearly countable, show exactly 16;
- if natural occlusion prevents exact counting, the composition must still plausibly represent the full cohort;
- do not show only 6–8 people;
- do not create a crowd larger than the stated cohort;
- do not make the visible group obviously gender-imbalanced.

## 7. Preferred camera geometry

`WIDE INTERIOR OBLIQUE ENTRANCE SHOT / GROUP MOVING INWARD`

Camera:

- inside the castle;
- several meters beyond the threshold;
- laterally offset;
- looking diagonally back toward the open entrance;
- enough width to reveal group scale;
- not centered symmetrically on the doorway.

The group should move through / beyond the threshold toward the camera's interior side or obliquely past it.

Avoid:

- frontal cast lineup;
- class-photo composition;
- fashion runway;
- military formation.

## 8. Depth composition

Use layered blocking.

### Fore / near-mid

- several guests already farther inside;
- Ning + Jun may be here;
- backs / rear-3Q / side-3Q;
- natural overlap.

### Mid

- Su + Liao loose cluster;
- Wen Qingya with one companion;
- additional unnamed guests;
- enough visibility for future recognition without spotlighting them.

### Rear-mid / threshold

- Guang Yong;
- remaining participants;
- still-open main doors behind them.

No layer should look like an arranged row.

## 9. Neil rule

Neil is NOT one of the sixteen participants.

Preferred N21 execution:

`NEIL NOT VISIBLE`

Reason:

- avoids count ambiguity;
- preserves N22 as Neil's next strong visual beat;
- keeps N21 focused on participant scale and character seeding.

Do not place a butler-like figure among the sixteen.

## 10. Movement language

Original action is sequential entry after participants locate their teammates.

Therefore:

- use small teammate clusters;
- some pairs / small groups naturally closer together;
- varied gait phases;
- no synchronized walking;
- no orderly single-file queue;
- no mass frontal pose;
- no one looking at camera.

Visual read:

`people who came with / found their teammates are filtering into the castle together`

## 11. Lighting

Maintain N20's interior logic.

- open entrance behind them may be visibly brighter;
- exterior daylight stays concentrated near the doorway;
- direct sunlight must not flood the foreground walking floor;
- interior remains primarily warm / dim;
- wall sconces / candles / chandeliers may define the interior;
- no rain;
- no storm foreshadowing.

Transition:

`BRIGHTER OPEN ENTRANCE BEHIND → DIM WARM INTERIOR AROUND GROUP`

## 12. Architecture / scene boundary

Scene:

`CASTLE_ENTRANCE → INTERIOR TRANSITION ZONE`

Allowed:

- open entrance doors;
- stone arches / pillars;
- limited interior depth;
- warm architectural lighting.

Not allowed:

- full First Hall reveal;
- A07-style establishment;
- mural corridor;
- long repetitive corridor;
- new architecture disconnected from the approved castle entrance.

N21 expands:

`PEOPLE SCALE`

not:

`BUILDING SCALE`

## 15. Character prominence ceiling

N21 must not become a six-character promotional ensemble.

Prominence order:

1. full sixteen-person cohort
2. architectural entry flow
3. Ning + Jun continuity
4. Su / Liao / Wen / Guang recognition
5. individual face readability

If stronger face visibility damages group realism:

`SHOW LESS FACE`

rather than staging the characters toward camera.

- Wen Qingya already confronting Ning;
- Guang Yong already asking Neil a question;
- Liao Jian / Su Xiaoxiao comedy beat;

- exact symmetrical 8+8 rows;

- rain;
- closed entrance doors;
- N22 guide-leading action.
