## 1. Story beat

Jun Luyuan nods and follows the others into the castle.

Narration establishes:

- total ghost guests / participants: `16`;
- men and women are evenly split;
- the visual focus expands from the preceding private Ning–Jun exchange to the full participant group.

This is the first S02-B shot whose main subject is:

`GROUP SCALE`

rather than an individual character.

## 2. Continuity from N20

N20 ends with Ning Qiushui and Jun Luyuan already moving slowly deeper into the castle.

N21 must NOT reset them to the exterior or back to a static doorway conversation.

Continuity progression:

`N20 two-person slow conversational walk → N21 widen out to reveal the larger cohort entering behind / around them`

Ning and Jun may remain identifiable within the larger group, but:

`THEY MUST NOT DOMINATE THE FRAME`

The visual center shifts from:

`NING + JUN`

to:

`THE FULL COHORT`

## 3. Preferred camera geometry

Preferred composition:

`WIDE INTERIOR OBLIQUE ENTRANCE SHOT / GROUP MOVING INWARD`

Camera:

- positioned inside the castle;
- several meters beyond the threshold;
- offset laterally rather than centered on the doorway;
- looks diagonally back toward the still-open entrance;
- sees the group moving from the bright entrance zone into the dimmer interior;
- avoids a symmetrical frontal crowd portrait.

The camera should feel observational, not staged.

The entrance is visible behind the cohort as spatial continuity, but the image must still read as:

`THE GROUP IS ENTERING THE CASTLE`

not:

`THE GROUP IS POSING AT THE DOOR`

## 4. Group blocking

Use layered depth instead of one horizontal row.

Preferred structure:

### Fore / near-mid layer

- several guests already just inside;
- backs / rear-3Q / side-3Q views;
- natural walking overlap;
- Ning and Jun may appear here or in the central layer but remain secondary.

### Central layer

- the main body of the cohort crossing or just beyond the threshold;
- mixed body orientations caused by natural walking;
- partial occlusion between people is expected.

### Rear layer

- remaining guests nearer the open doorway;
- some figures may be partially cropped or occluded by other guests / architecture.

This layered structure should make the cohort feel real and mobile rather than arranged for counting.

## 5. Sixteen-person count rule

Canonical story fact:

`16 GUESTS TOTAL / 8 MEN + 8 WOMEN`

The image must not visibly contradict this.

Preferred visual strategy:

- build a cohort that reads approximately as the complete sixteen-person group;
- distribute men and women across all depth layers;
- avoid an obviously male-heavy or female-heavy group;
- use natural partial occlusion so the frame does not become a rigid counting diagram.

Hard review rule:

- if every figure is clearly countable, the total must be `16`;
- if some figures are naturally partially occluded / cropped, the frame must still plausibly read as the full sixteen-person cohort;
- an obviously wrong visible scale such as only 6–8 people or a crowd of 20+ is a FAIL;
- do not duplicate one face / hairstyle repeatedly to manufacture the count.

Gender balance:

`8 male / 8 female at cohort level`

must remain the intended composition truth.

## 6. Neil exclusion

Neil is NOT one of the sixteen ghost guests.

For N21, preferred execution is:

`NEIL NOT VISIBLE`

This completely avoids count ambiguity.

Neil receives his own guide-leading beat in N22.

Do not place a butler-like figure inside the sixteen-person group.

## 7. Ning / Jun treatment

Ning Qiushui and Jun Luyuan:

- may be recognizable within the cohort;
- may occupy a front / near-mid position because N20 has already moved them inward;
- must not return to a hero two-shot;
- must not resume shoulder contact;
- must not stop to talk;
- must not look toward camera;
- must continue the general inward movement.

Identity should be preserved if visible, but N21 is not an identity close-up.

## 8. Other guest treatment

The remaining guests should read as distinct adult individuals.

Requirements:

- mixed male / female;
- varied hairstyles;
- varied but grounded contemporary clothing;
- restrained colors compatible with the existing Black Lady visual world;
- varied height / body proportions;
- no obvious clones;
- no identical jackets repeated across multiple people;
- no fashion-runway staging;
- no exaggerated fear or panic.

The term “ghost guests” is narrative classification only.

Do NOT visualize them as:

- transparent spirits;
- glowing ghosts;
- corpses;
- supernatural silhouettes.

They should appear as ordinary living-looking participants at this moment.

## 9. Lighting

The open entrance behind the group may remain visibly brighter than the interior.

However:

- daylight must stay concentrated around / near the doorway;
- do not flood the entire interior floor with hard exterior sunlight;
- foreground / midground interior remains governed mainly by warm wall sconces / candles / chandeliers;
- light transition should read naturally from cooler / brighter entrance to dim warm interior;
- no storm / rain / supernatural light.

This preserves N20's established interior-lighting logic while allowing N21 to show the still-open door.

## 10. Architecture / spatial boundary

Scene remains:

`CASTLE_ENTRANCE → INTERIOR TRANSITION ZONE`

Allowed:

- open main doors;
- entrance stone architecture;
- pillars / arches;
- warm wall lights;
- limited interior depth.

Not allowed:

- full First Hall establishment;
- A07-style hall reveal;
- mural corridor;
- long repetitive hallway;
- new castle architecture inconsistent with the established entrance.

N21 expands GROUP SCALE, not SCENE SCALE.

## 11. Movement language

The group is entering naturally.

Use:

- different gait phases;
- varied but coherent walking speeds;
- partial overlaps;
- small head turns between nearby participants;
- natural spacing.

Avoid:

- sixteen people walking in perfect synchronized formation;
- military march;
- symmetrical pairs;
- orderly queue;
- posed class photo;
- everyone facing camera.

The image must feel like multiple people filtering inward after entry.
