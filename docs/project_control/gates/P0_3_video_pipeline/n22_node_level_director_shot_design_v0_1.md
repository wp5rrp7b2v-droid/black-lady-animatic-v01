## 1. GOAL

Create one Story Shot that turns the narration's abstract fact—

`16 ghost guests / men and women evenly split`

—into a human-scale observational beat.

The viewer should not read:

`sixteen anonymous people entering`

but instead:

`there are individual people inside this group, and some of them may matter later`.

N22 is therefore not a second N21 crowd-scale shot.

It is a selective observational fragment attributed to Ning Qiushui.

---

## 2. SUCCESS

N22 succeeds if the frame simultaneously reads as:

1. a fragment of a larger mixed group;
2. Ning-attributed observation rather than neutral group photography;
3. informal early character seeding, not formal introduction;
4. natural, asynchronous human presence rather than lineup / queue;
5. the same dim warm entrance-to-interior visual world established by N20;
6. a visual bridge toward N23 without already showing Neil's direction change.

---

## 3. EVIDENCE CLASS

### FACT

- Parent source scope: `01:54.700 → 02:04.300`.
- S3 searchable audio index places the narration approximately at `01:55.000 → 02:07.500`: Jun nods, follows the group inside; there are sixteen ghost guests, men and women evenly split.
- N22's locked V0.3 function is `POV + INFORMATION UPGRADE + NAMED CHARACTER SEEDING`.
- N22 does not need to prove all sixteen bodies.
- Named-character seeding candidates already established at V0.3 level:
  - Su Xiaoxiao;
  - Liao Jian;
  - Wen Qingya;
  - Guang Yong.
- N20 approved visual world:
  - dim warm interior sources;
  - limited entrance-to-interior transition;
  - no flooding direct exterior sunlight;
  - no full-hall establishment.

### INFERENCE

- Showing a partial group fragment is more useful than a second wide crowd shot because N22's narrative function is individualization, not scale.
- Differential prominence is required: not all named characters should receive equal visual weight.

### WORKING HYPOTHESIS

A single 9:16 frame can seed four named identities if only two are relatively readable and the other two are secondary / partially occluded, while unnamed guests provide group context.

This is not yet proven by generation.

### UNKNOWN

- Exact final edit duration inside the parent beat.
- Whether four named seeds can remain visually stable in one generated frame without creating a posed ensemble.
- Final reference count required by the N22 Bundle.

## 5. CAMERA / COMPOSITION DESIGN

Target format:

`9:16 vertical`

Shot scale:

`MEDIUM-WIDE GROUP FRAGMENT`

Camera relationship:

`NING-ATTRIBUTED NEAR-POV / OBSERVATIONAL`

### Foreground

At one lateral edge only:

- a cropped, soft-focus portion of Ning Qiushui's shoulder / back-of-head may be present;
- it should occupy only a small edge area;
- it is a POV cue, not a protagonist portrait;
- no visible frontal Ning face is required.

If Ning's edge presence materially increases generation instability, it may be removed while preserving POV attribution through edit continuity.

### Midground primary seeds

Use one loose, non-posed relationship:

#### Su Xiaoxiao

- feminine-presenting;
- naturally integrated into the moving group;
- no future reveal foreshadowing;
- no deliberate glamour pose;
- not looking into camera.

#### Liao Jian

- fit young man;
- ordinary athletic build, not bodybuilding;
- positioned near Su Xiaoxiao but not as a couple portrait;
- body orientation / gait phase different from Su Xiaoxiao.

Su + Liao may form the most readable human cluster in N22.

### Secondary depth seeds

#### Wen Qingya

- separate from Su / Liao;
- round-frame glasses may be readable if natural;
- cultured / composed impression;
- partially overlapped by another person or architecture is acceptable.

#### Guang Yong

- separate depth layer;
- shorter / slightly chubby silhouette;
- need not present a full clean face;
- partial occlusion is preferred over lineup clarity.

### Unnamed guests

Several additional unnamed human presences may appear through:

- cropped shoulders;
- half bodies;
- soft depth figures;
- partial silhouettes.

They exist only to communicate:

`THIS FRAGMENT BELONGS TO A LARGER GROUP`

No hard visible-person count is required.

## 6. BLOCKING

Global movement:

`GENERAL INWARD FLOW`

Local behavior:

`ASYNCHRONOUS / NON-CHOREOGRAPHED`

People should not:

- walk in one file;
- share the same stride phase;
- turn their heads in the same direction;
- line up shoulder-to-shoulder;
- face camera as a cast photo;
- form a symmetric V / arc / row.

Acceptable local variation:

- one person still moving;
- one person briefly looking around;
- one person partly paused;
- one person obscured behind another;
- uneven lateral spacing;
- differing body angles.

The frame should feel observed in passing, not arranged.

---

## 7. SPACE

N22 remains inside:

`CASTLE ENTRANCE → INTERIOR TRANSITION ZONE`

Do not spend:

- First Hall full establishment;
- deep corridor reveal;
- monumental portal reveal.

Architecture should be supporting structure, not subject.

Preferred:

- off-axis stone wall / column;
- partial arch fragment;
- broken depth;
- layered occlusion;
- non-symmetrical background;
- limited darker continuation beyond the group.

Avoid:

- giant centered Gothic arch;
- large symmetrical double doors;
- strong centered vanishing point;
- cathedral-scale interior showcase;
- clean ceremonial entrance composition.

The open exterior doorway may be absent or reduced to a minor cool light trace.

---

## 8. LIGHT / COLOR CONTINUITY

Match the approved N20 visual world.

Lock:

- dim warm amber / tungsten interior practical light;
- natural skin, not orange;
- low-key exposure;
- readable faces / silhouettes;
- restrained saturation;
- moderate cinematic contrast;
- textured dark clothing and stone;
- no exterior daylight flooding the floor or backs.

The image must look like the same place and same time as N20.

## 12. CRITICAL ASSUMPTION

Critical unverified assumption:

`FOUR NAMED CHARACTER SEEDS CAN COEXIST IN ONE FRAME WITHOUT IDENTITY DRIFT OR LINEUP COMPOSITION`

If this assumption fails, N22 must not enter repeated blind regeneration.

Fallback design boundary:

- retain Su Xiaoxiao + Liao Jian as primary seeds;
- retain only one of Wen Qingya / Guang Yong as secondary readable seed;
- defer the remaining character's visual seeding to a later compatible node.

This fallback does not violate the V0.3 node function because V0.3 does not require all four named characters to be visibly proven in N22.

## 13. MINIMUM PROOF

First generation test, after later Scene Reference + Bundle approval:

`1 × Candidate only`

Do not batch.

### PASS

Candidate may proceed to Product Owner review if:

1. it reads as an observed fragment of a larger group;
2. Su Xiaoxiao and Liao Jian are recognizable without hero posing;
3. Wen Qingya and Guang Yong remain distinguishable enough for early seeding, even with partial occlusion;
4. unnamed people provide group context without forming a queue;
5. no one is arranged into a formal cast lineup;
6. architecture remains subordinate and off-axis;
7. no monumental symmetrical Gothic drift;
8. light / color visibly belongs to the N20 world;
9. Neil does not dominate;
10. N22 does not consume N23's direction-change beat.

### FAIL

Stop and redesign if any of the following occurs:

- multi-character identity collapse;
- all four named characters become a posed ensemble;
- people become one-file / ceremonial entry;
- central-axis crowd composition returns;
- architecture becomes the primary spectacle;
- warm-low-key continuity is lost;
- exact-count obsession becomes visually obvious;
- Neil becomes the shot subject;
- the frame effectively duplicates N21.
