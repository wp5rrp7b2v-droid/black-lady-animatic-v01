## 1. Source fact

`02:15.000–02:19.500`

Neil:

`城堡大门只会在下雨天关闭`

Novel action:

`尼尔管家听到这话，头也不回地淡淡道`

Therefore the governing action is:

`NEIL CONTINUES MOVING INWARD / DOES NOT TURN BACK / ANSWERS CALMLY`

The old V0.3 provisional example "rear-3Q turn-back" is superseded for N25 exact composition.

## 2. Narrative function

`INFORMATION UPGRADE + RULE DELIVERY`

N25 converts the A06 question into a concrete rule:

`THE CASTLE DOOR CLOSES ONLY WHEN IT RAINS`

The shot should feel understated, not explanatory or theatrical.

## 3. Continuity from A06

A06 already establishes:

- Neil has left the doorway and is moving inward;
- the main door remains open;
- someone behind / within the group notices and asks about it.

N25 begins immediately after that question.

No reset to the doorway.

No new entrance establishment.

No spatial jump to a distant hall.

Neil is now naturally somewhat farther inward than in N23, but still within the same continuous walking action.

## 4. First-read hierarchy

First read:

`NEIL KEEPS WALKING AND ANSWERS WITHOUT TURNING BACK`

Second read:

`THE GROUP IS FOLLOWING / LISTENING WHILE MOVING`

Third read:

`THE ANSWER IS STRANGELY CASUAL GIVEN THE UNUSUAL RULE`

Do not make first read:

- Neil hero portrait;
- Neil confronting the group;
- Neil stopping to give a formal explanation;
- another open-door detail shot.

## 5. Camera / composition

Target:

`9:16 VERTICAL`

Preferred framing:

`MEDIUM / MEDIUM-WIDE REAR-3Q`

Camera should be behind or slightly lateral to the moving group.

Neil:

- midground or forward midground;
- back / rear-3Q / limited profile reveal;
- body direction stays inward;
- head does not turn back toward the group;
- mouth may be only minimally visible if naturally exposed by rear-3Q;
- speaking must feel casual and continuous with walking.

The shot does not require a clear frontal lip-reading view.

## 6. Group presence

Use only enough people to prove the answer is being delivered while the group moves.

Preferred:

- 2–4 partial / staggered followers;
- foreground shoulders / side-backs acceptable;
- no lineup;
- no synchronized gait;
- no need to identify all named characters.

Ning does not need to be dominant in N25.

N26 will return the sequence to Ning's reaction.

## 7. Space

N25 remains in the transition route between Castle Entrance and First Hall.

Allowed:

- entrance-adjacent interior continuation;
- short corridor / passage cues consistent with Castle Entrance spatial family;
- restrained forward depth.

Not allowed:

- full First Hall establishment;
- fireplace;
- formal hall reveal;
- deep unknown threshold climax.

The First Hall introduction begins immediately after S02-B, so N25 must not spend that reveal early.

## 8. Door visibility

The door itself is NOT required in N25.

A06 has just established it.

Preferred:

`DO NOT FORCE THE OPEN DOOR BACK INTO FRAME`

This avoids visual repetition.

Continuity may instead survive through light direction / recent edit memory.

## 9. Neil performance

Required emotional register:

`CALM / ROUTINE / MATTER-OF-FACT`

He says the unusual rule as though it is ordinary.

No:

- warning gesture;
- ominous smile emphasis;
- dramatic pause pose;
- eye contact with camera;
- full body turn;
- pointing back at door.

This restraint is the unsettling part of the beat.

## 10. Light / color

Continue the current S02-B interior family:

- warm low-key interior;
- restrained amber / tungsten practicals;
- natural skin;
- deep but textured blacks;
- low-to-moderate saturation;
- clarity without raising overall brightness;
- no dirty noise;
- no cutout edges.

- Neil faces camera;
- Neil stops for a speech;
- exterior welcome composition;
- rain shown;
- door closes;
- full First Hall;
- fireplace establishment;
- heroic Neil framing;
- backpack / shoulder bag / crossbody bag / luggage;
- queue / equal spacing / synchronized walking;
- bright studio lighting;
- dirty / noisy / pasted-on look.
