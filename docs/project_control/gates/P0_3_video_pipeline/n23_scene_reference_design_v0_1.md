## 1. SCENE REFERENCE OBJECTIVE

Lock the camera-space relationship before Bundle construction so Work cannot misread the direction of movement.

The Scene Reference must establish:

`CAMERA INSIDE CASTLE → DIAGONALLY LOOKING BACK TOWARD OPEN ENTRANCE`

with:

- Neil standing beside the open door;
- the same N22 cohort continuing inward;
- group movement reading unequivocally as `ENTRANCE → CASTLE INTERIOR`;
- no accidental read of people leaving the castle;
- 9:16 vertical composition logic.

## 3. CAMERA POSITION — LOCKED

Camera is:

- inside the castle;
- already beyond the threshold;
- positioned slightly deeper inside than the entering people;
- offset to one side of the group's forward route;
- diagonally looking back toward the entrance;
- not placed on the central doorway axis.

The camera must read as observing people who are continuing past it / into the interior, not as facing a group that is walking out.

Locked spatial statement:

`CAMERA = INTERIOR / DEEPER SIDE POSITION`

`LENS DIRECTION = BACK TOWARD OPEN DOOR`

`GROUP MOTION = DOOR → INTERIOR / TOWARD DEEPER CASTLE SPACE`

---

## 4. HARD MOVEMENT-DIRECTION RULE

The first-glance movement read must be:

`PEOPLE ARE ENTERING THE CASTLE`

Never:

`PEOPLE ARE LEAVING THE CASTLE`

Visual implementation should prefer:

- backs;
- side-backs;
- partial backs;
- mixed 3/4 body angles consistent with inward travel.

If a generated frame can reasonably be read as the cohort walking outside, it is an automatic FAIL.

---

## 5. FRAME ORGANIZATION

### Background / upper-depth zone

- open castle door;
- only limited exterior brightness;
- no white portal;
- no broad daylight spill.

### Door-side anchor

- Neil stands at one side of the open door;
- not centered;
- not touching the door;
- not closing it;
- not yet walking away;
- side glance toward the entering cohort.

### Midground

- same N22 cohort continues inward;
- natural overlap;
- staggered depth;
- different stride phases.

### Foreground / nearer interior

- partial entering figures may crop the frame;
- people may pass nearer camera;
- no clean row / queue.

This should create a clear depth chain:

`OPEN DOOR → NEIL AT SIDE → ENTERING COHORT → DEEPER INTERIOR / CAMERA SIDE`

## 10. LIGHTING / COLOR

Maintain N22 continuity:

- warm amber / tungsten practicals;
- low-key interior;
- natural skin tones;
- readable black-level texture;
- restrained saturation;
- moderate cinematic contrast.

Because the lens looks back toward the entrance, slightly more exterior luminance is acceptable, but:

- no blown doorway;
- no bright white portal;
- no broad light band across floor;
- no large daylight-dominant backlight.

## 14. PASS / FAIL TEST

### PASS

Scene reference logic is preserved if:

1. frame is designed for 9:16;
2. camera is unmistakably inside;
3. lens looks diagonally back toward entrance;
4. people unmistakably move inward;
5. Neil stands beside open door;
6. N22 cohort continuity is believable;
7. Guang Yong cue remains tertiary if used;
8. A06 action/question is not consumed.

### FAIL

Automatic fail if:

- people read as walking outside;
- camera appears outside the castle;
- horizontal-composition logic dominates;
- Neil is centered as hero;
- Neil closes / touches door;
- Neil has already walked away;
- N22 is mirrored literally;
- group queues or synchronizes;
- exterior light dominates.
