---

## 1. REDESIGN TRIGGER

N21 Candidate 01–08 repeatedly failed when the shot attempted to combine:

- visible cohort scale;
- 4–6 readable full people;
- entrance architecture;
- inward movement;
- natural crowd blocking;
- unknown-space mood;

inside one wide / medium-wide Story Shot.

Candidate 08 confirms that body / wardrobe / movement-direction control can work.

The remaining repeated failure is strongly associated with the shot being organized as:

`GROUP + ENTRANCE + DEEP SPACE = CENTRAL PROCESSION / ARCHITECTURAL SHOWCASE`

Therefore this redesign changes the shot function itself rather than adding more prompt rules.

---

## 2. NEW N21 FUNCTION

Previous emphasis:

`FULL COHORT SCALE REVEAL`

New emphasis:

`IMMERSIVE SCALE SHIFT + THRESHOLD TRANSITION`

N21 no longer has to visually prove the complete sixteen-person cohort.

The audio fact:

`16 participants / men and women evenly split`

continues to belong primarily to N22's observational / information beat.

N21 only has to make the audience feel:

`THE PRIVATE TWO-PERSON MOVEMENT HAS BECOME PART OF A LARGER HUMAN FLOW`

This is sufficient narrative value.

---

## 3. EDITORIAL POSITION BETWEEN APPROVED SHOTS

### N20
`NING + JUN / PRIVATE TWO-PERSON INWARD MOVEMENT`

### NEW N21
`VIEWER IS NOW INSIDE / BESIDE THE MOVING COHORT`

### N22
`NING-ATTRIBUTED OBSERVATION PICKS OUT INDIVIDUAL PEOPLE FROM THE GROUP`

### N23
`CUT BACK TO NEIL AT THE OPEN DOOR AS THE TAIL OF THE GROUP PASSES`

Therefore N21 must not duplicate:

- N22's named-character / individual-person emphasis;
- N23's Neil + open-door composition.

---

## 4. CAMERA CONCEPT

Primary camera concept:

`AMONG-THE-GROUP / SHOULDER-HEIGHT / OBLIQUE / OFF-AXIS`

The camera should feel physically close to the moving participants rather than standing back to photograph the group.

Preferred framing:

- medium-wide to medium;
- one near figure may be strongly cropped by frame edge;
- one or two overlapping midground bodies;
- one or two deeper figures;
- irregular depth;
- no complete architectural symmetry.

The camera does NOT need to show the full entrance.

The camera does NOT need to prove where every person came from.

---

## 5. HUMAN COUNT / CROWD COMPLEXITY

Readable requirement:

`3–5 HUMAN BODIES / BODY FRAGMENTS ARE ENOUGH`

This is intentionally lower than the old 4–6 full-readable-person target.

Allowed:

- cropped shoulders;
- partial backs;
- half bodies at frame edge;
- overlapping figures;
- deeper silhouettes;
- one person substantially occluding another.

The larger cohort is implied by:

`LAYERING + CROPPING + OCCLUSION + CONTINUOUS FLOW`

not by counting bodies.

If only four people are clearly readable, that is acceptable.

N21 does not have to make sixteen people visually countable.

---

## 6. CROWD BEHAVIOR

Movement:

`THRESHOLD → DEEPER CASTLE INTERIOR`

But composition should not organize the people into a visible route diagram.

Required:

- different depths;
- unequal gaps;
- different gait phases;
- body overlap;
- small local facing variation;
- one shared general inward direction.

Preferred feeling:

`CAUGHT MID-MOVEMENT / VIEWER STANDING BESIDE OR WITHIN THE FLOW`

Forbidden:

- single-file line;
- front-two / middle-two / rear-one formation;
- V formation;
- equal spacing;
- visible leader;
- synchronized strides;
- everyone fully separated and readable.

---

## 7. ARCHITECTURE POLICY

N21 is no longer an entrance-architecture shot.

Architecture should function as:

`PARTIAL SPATIAL PRESSURE / NOT SUBJECT`

Allowed:

- cropped stone wall;
- partial column;
- doorway edge;
- local warm sconce;
- dark opening / turn ahead;
- incomplete interior fragments.

Do not show:

- complete grand arch;
- full double-door geometry;
- long clean corridor;
- repeated arches forming a perspective tunnel;
- centered vanishing point;
- symmetrical Gothic showcase;
- complete First Hall.

The viewer should know:

`THE GROUP IS MOVING FARTHER INSIDE`

without being shown:

`A COMPLETE MAP OF THE SPACE AHEAD`

---

## 8. UNKNOWN-SPACE MOOD

Unknown-space feeling should come from:

- near-body occlusion;
- incomplete architecture;
- darkness beyond the readable figures;
- diminishing practical light;
- route ahead partly hidden by bodies / wall / column;
- visual information ending before the space is fully explained.

Do NOT create uncertainty using:

- heavy fog;
- supernatural glow;
- horror-game blue lighting;
- fantasy effects.

Threat remains latent.

---

## 9. DOOR / NEIL POLICY

Neil:

`NOT VISIBLE`

The main entrance door:

`MAY BE ABSENT OR ONLY A SMALL / PARTIAL REAR CUE`

N21 does not need to preserve a readable full doorway.

Reason:

- N20 already establishes continuous inward movement;
- N23 later owns Neil + open-door story information;
- forcing the door into N21 repeatedly pushes the image back toward architecture-first composition.

---

## 10. NAMED CHARACTER POLICY

N21 remains:

`GROUP FIRST / NO NAMED-CHARACTER PRESENTATION TASK`

Ning Qiushui / Jun Luyuan do not need to be clearly identifiable in N21.

This is a deliberate change from earlier N21 concepts.

Continuity from N20 is editorial:

`THEY HAVE JOINED THE FLOW`

not dependent on clearly displaying both of them again.

Named-character reading returns in N22.

---

## 11. LIGHT / COLOR

Continuity family:

`N20 → N21 → N22 → N23 = SAME CASTLE / SAME CONTINUOUS PERIOD`

Target:

- warm low-key interior;
- natural skin;
- restrained saturation;
- readable dark fabric / hair / stone;
- moderate contrast;
- deeper interior gradually darker.

Exterior light:

`OPTIONAL / MINIMAL / NON-DOMINANT`

No:

- broad daylight floor reflection;
- large blue-sky opening;
- bright portal centerpiece;
- orange global wash;
- blue horror reset.

---

## 12. VISUAL CHANGE FROM N20

N20:

- two identifiable people;
- relatively readable full-body movement;
- private conversational relationship.

N21 must change at least:

1. `SUBJECT SCALE` — pair → anonymous human flow;
2. `CAMERA RELATION` — observing two people → physically inside / beside the group;
3. `OCCLUSION` — cleaner two-person readability → layered partial bodies;
4. `SPATIAL INFORMATION` — known entrance transition → less-readable deeper interior.

This makes N21 worth existing as a separate visual node.

---

## 13. VISUAL HANDOFF TO N22

N21 should end visually with:

`A MOVING GROUP THAT IS NOT YET INDIVIDUALIZED`

Then N22 can legitimately change function to:

`NING NOTICES PARTICULAR PEOPLE INSIDE THAT GROUP`

Therefore N21 should avoid readable named faces and avoid clean character presentation.

This creates the intended editorial progression:

`CROWD → INDIVIDUALS`

---

## 14. HARD FAIL CONDITIONS

Automatic Director FAIL if:

- frame reads as a group entering a grand cathedral / Gothic hall;
- architecture is more important than people;
- clear centered vanishing point dominates;
- people form a queue / organized procession;
- all bodies are neatly separated;
- shot becomes a group photograph;
- full door / full arch is heroically displayed;
- named characters are deliberately presented;
- Neil is visible;
- broad exterior daylight dominates;
- prohibited bags / luggage appear;
- body proportions regress;
- visual style becomes commercial / fashion / game NPC.

---

## 15. PASS TEST

N21 V0.2 succeeds if:

1. first read = `WE ARE NOW INSIDE A MOVING GROUP`;
2. transition from N20's two-person beat is immediately perceptible;
3. only 3–5 readable bodies / fragments are sufficient;
4. natural overlap and cropping make the group feel larger than the visible count;
5. no queue / formation / central leader axis;
6. architecture is incomplete and subordinate;
7. deeper space remains partly unreadable;
8. no full entrance or First Hall reveal is required;
9. warm low-key continuity remains intact;
10. N22 can naturally follow by isolating individual people from this anonymous flow.

---
