## 1. GOAL

Show the first clear spatial action after N22:

`NEIL TURNS AND THE GROUP'S TRAVEL DIRECTION CHANGES`

The shot must add a genuine spatial event, not merely another image of people walking.

N23 exists to tell the viewer:

`THE GROUP IS NOW BEING LED SOMEWHERE ELSE INSIDE THE CASTLE`

---

## 2. SUCCESS

N23 succeeds if the frame simultaneously communicates:

1. Neil has just turned / is actively changing travel direction;
2. the nearest part of the group is beginning to follow that new direction;
3. the change is asynchronous rather than choreographed;
4. the viewer can read a bend / redirection in movement without needing a full hall reveal;
5. the image remains inside the same warm low-key castle world as N22;
6. Neil functions as the guide for this action without becoming the sequence protagonist;
7. N24 still has room to own Ning's deeper-interior POV.

## 6. CAMERA DESIGN

Target format:

`9:16 vertical`

Shot scale:

`MEDIUM-WIDE SPATIAL GROUP SHOT`

Camera relation:

`REAR / REAR-3Q / LATERALLY OFFSET`

Preferred camera position:

- inside the castle;
- slightly behind and to one side of the cohort;
- enough lateral offset to make the path bend readable;
- eye level or only slightly elevated;
- not centered on a grand architectural axis.

The frame should allow the viewer to see:

`OLD GROUP VECTOR → NEIL'S NEW VECTOR`

without diagrammatic staging.

---

## 7. NEIL BLOCKING

Neil is:

`ACTION ANCHOR / GUIDE`

not:

`HERO PORTRAIT`

Preferred state:

- farther ahead than most people;
- body already rotated into the new route;
- rear or rear-3Q readable;
- one foot / weight transfer beginning the new direction;
- restrained head turn consistent with leading;
- no need for exaggerated hand gesture;
- no formal speech pose.

Neil should be clearly identifiable as the person initiating the movement change, but should not occupy the largest or brightest face area.

---

## 8. GROUP BLOCKING

Key rule:

`DIRECTION CHANGE MUST BE ASYNCHRONOUS`

The group should not all pivot at once.

Preferred distribution:

- 1–2 nearest people have already begun changing direction;
- some middle figures are between old and new vectors;
- some deeper / rear figures are still finishing their prior movement;
- body angles and stride phases differ;
- heads do not all turn toward Neil simultaneously.

This creates a natural propagation of movement through the group.

Avoid:

- synchronized military turn;
- single-file queue;
- shoulder-to-shoulder row;
- everyone facing exactly the new direction;
- cast-photo spacing;
- central procession.

## 10. SPACE DESIGN

Location remains:

`CASTLE INTERIOR TRANSITION ZONE`

The shot needs a readable new route but not a full destination.

Preferred spatial cues:

- one side opening / turn / darker lateral continuation;
- stone column or wall edge that helps imply a bend;
- layered depth with partial architectural occlusion;
- the new route becoming slightly darker than the current area.

Avoid:

- full First Hall establishment;
- giant central arch;
- cathedral-scale symmetry;
- huge centered double doors;
- perfect central vanishing point;
- exterior entrance as main background subject.

The open entrance may be absent or only a minor distant edge cue.

---

## 11. LIGHT / COLOR CONTINUITY

Carry forward the current S02-B interior look:

- warm amber / tungsten practical-light family;
- natural skin tones;
- low-key exposure;
- readable texture in dark clothing, hair and stone;
- restrained saturation;
- moderate cinematic contrast;
- no heavy orange cast;
- no exterior daylight flooding the floor.

N23 may be slightly darker than N22 if the camera has turned farther inward.

That is a story-motivated shift, not a grading discontinuity.

---

## 12. MOVEMENT READ

The frame must communicate a bend in direction without looking staged.

Useful visual cues may include:

- Neil's torso / leg vector pointing into the new route;
- one nearby person's shoulders beginning to follow;
- another person's body still on the previous vector;
- architectural edge reinforcing the bend;
- unequal spacing caused by the turn.

The image should feel captured during transition.

Do not freeze everyone into equally balanced positions.

---

### PASS

Candidate may proceed to Product Owner review if:

1. Neil is identifiable as guide/action anchor;
2. Neil is visibly initiating a change of direction;
3. at least two different movement phases are readable in the group;
4. the crowd does not turn synchronously;
5. the new route is legible without full hall reveal;
6. architecture remains subordinate;
7. N22's people-seeding composition is not repeated;
8. N24's POV function is not consumed;
9. lighting belongs to the same castle/time;
10. no bags / luggage appear.

### FAIL

Stop and redesign if:

- everyone turns together;
- crowd becomes a queue;
- Neil becomes a portrait subject;
- frame is dominated by architecture;
- full destination is revealed;
- image looks like another generic walking shot;
- N22 composition is effectively repeated;
- N24 POV is prematurely used.
