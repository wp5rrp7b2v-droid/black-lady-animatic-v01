# N11｜Neil Abnormal Portrait｜Director Shot Design V0

Status: `DRAFT / PRODUCT OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED / NOT YET A STORY SHOT`

Sequence: S02-A｜观察尼尔与钥匙判断

## 1. Narrative function

N11 begins the post-Opening scrutiny of Neil.

It corresponds to the narrative information:

- Neil's skin is extremely pale, without healthy color;
- he appears to be about fifty years old.

The shot must shift the audience from "Neil as a formal welcoming butler" to "Neil as a person Ning Qiushui is now carefully evaluating."

This is an observation shot, not a new action beat.

## 2. Story position

Immediately after approved `OPENING_V001`.

Continuity state:

- castle entrance / threshold continuity remains;
- Neil has completed the welcome and is waiting for the visitors;
- no procession into the castle has begun in this shot;
- no new turn / step / hand action;
- the visual language becomes more observational and uneasy.

## 3. Composition

Recommended framing:

- vertical 9:16 Story Shot;
- chest-up / medium close-up;
- Neil is the sole clear subject;
- camera near eye level;
- slight three-quarter angle rather than a flat ID-photo front view;
- head and upper torso large enough to read pallor and age;
- retain some castle-entrance environment behind him so the shot remains spatially connected to Opening;
- background secondary / restrained, not a new environment-establishing shot.

Preferred visual hierarchy:

`Neil face → pale skin / age → formal black suit → castle entrance context`

## 4. Performance

Neil:

- upright, formal butler posture;
- still and controlled;
- mouth closed;
- expression neutral-to-faintly-stiff;
- no friendly smile;
- no overt horror face;
- no speaking pose;
- no obvious turn or step.

The unease should come from subtle human abnormality, especially pallor and stillness, not from monsterization.

## 5. Appearance continuity

Must preserve canonical Neil identity and costume.

Required continuity:

- same face / age structure as approved Neil master references;
- same hair and grooming;
- same formal black butler suit / white shirt styling already established in Opening;
- skin should be clearly pale, but not corpse-blue, chalk-white, overexposed, or supernatural makeup;
- approximately fifty-year-old appearance must remain readable.

The cross may be incidentally visible if composition naturally includes it, but N11 must not make the cross the focal point. N12 owns the cross + rigid-smile emphasis.

## 6. Lighting / tone

- exterior / entrance continuity;
- subdued cool-natural atmosphere;
- preserve dimensional facial detail;
- pale skin must remain readable without blown highlights;
- brown / dark castle context may remain softly visible;
- restrained uncanny mood, not stylized horror-poster lighting.

## 8. Hard negatives / exclusions

Do not show:

- open mouth or active speaking;
- broad / friendly smile;
- exaggerated rigid grin reserved for N12;
- turn / step / walking action;
- hand gesture as a narrative focus;
- keys or waist inspection;
- waist close-up;
- obvious supernatural transformation;
- corpse / zombie styling;
- Dutch angle or sensational horror-poster composition;
- unrelated visitors dominating the frame.

## 9. Relationship to adjacent shots

Previous:
`OPENING_V001` ends with Neil waiting for the visitors.

N11:
`audience begins scrutinizing Neil himself`.

Next N12:
`attention tightens to cross + unnaturally rigid smile`.

Therefore N11 should establish the person; N12 should provide the more specific disturbing detail.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

N11 is acceptable only if:

1. Neil identity is stable;
2. entrance continuity is clear;
3. his pallor and approximate age read immediately;
4. the shot feels observational rather than like another welcome/speaking shot;
5. the mood is subtly wrong but not exaggerated;
6. N12 still has meaningful room to escalate to cross + rigid smile;
7. no key/waist information is leaked early.
