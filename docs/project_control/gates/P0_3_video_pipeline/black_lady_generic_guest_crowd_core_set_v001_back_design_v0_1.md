## 1. Purpose

Create the same approved Guest A–J identities from a full BACK orientation.

Core rule:

`THE SAME TEN PEOPLE FROM ANOTHER VIEW`

This is the fourth and final orientation board in V001.

Orientation sequence:

`FRONT 0° → LEFT_PROFILE 90° → REAR_3Q approximately 135° → BACK 180°`

## 2. BACK definition

BACK means:

- full back view;
- first-glance read = BACK;
- approximately 180° from FRONT;
- head and torso naturally aligned away from camera.

It is not:

- REAR_3Q;
- true side profile;
- over-the-shoulder portrait;
- deliberate face reveal;
- Story Shot blocking.

A tiny natural head asymmetry is acceptable only if the first-glance read remains unmistakably BACK.

## 4. Authority hierarchy

Primary global identity authority:

`BLACK_LADY_GENERIC_GUEST_CROWD_CORE_SET_V001_FRONT`

Secondary side-view continuity authority:

`BLACK_LADY_GENERIC_GUEST_CROWD_CORE_SET_V001_LEFT_PROFILE`

Secondary rear-side continuity authority:

`BLACK_LADY_GENERIC_GUEST_CROWD_CORE_SET_V001_REAR_3Q`

Conflict rule:

`FRONT WINS`

FRONT controls global identity-defining traits:

- identity;
- apparent age;
- body build;
- hairstyle identity;
- garment identity;
- footwear;
- identity-defining body-marking family.

LEFT_PROFILE may support side geometry.

REAR_3Q may support rear-side silhouette, shoulder/back geometry, hair fall and rear garment continuity.

Neither secondary authority may override FRONT on global identity traits.

## 5. Guest I hard lock

Guest I must remain:

- male;
- clearly muscular;
- black sleeveless top;
- short stiff / brush-cut hairstyle;
- identity-defining tattoo/body-marking family where naturally visible from the back.

The LEFT_PROFILE and REAR_3Q accepted tattoo rendering differences are orientation/rendering tolerances only.

They must not be treated as new tattoo designs.

Do not reduce Guest I to a generic black-sleeveless male.

## 6. Back-view continuity

BACK should complete information not fully visible in FRONT:

- rear head silhouette;
- rear hair fall;
- shoulder/back width;
- garment back construction;
- rear trouser silhouette;
- heel / shoe-back geometry.

Use minimum-new-information interpretation where exact rear details are not directly proven by existing authorities.

Do not invent distinctive new identity traits.
