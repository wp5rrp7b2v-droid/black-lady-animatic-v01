## 3. Visual findings

### N08

N08 correctly proves that the castle entrance is large and architecturally important.

However, it is an exterior architectural hero view.

If its visual logic is over-emphasized for N21, the shot risks becoming:

- monumental;
- symmetrical;
- architecture-first;
- cathedral-like.

This is not the desired N21 storytelling priority.

### Current Scene Master AST_IMG_000052

The Scene Master correctly preserves:

- open-door state;
- stone entrance;
- large doorway;
- warm interior sconces.

But as a direct generation image it also contains:

- a centered, symmetrical doorway;
- a large bright blue-sky opening;
- strong exterior daylight contrast.

Candidate 06 demonstrates that prompt instructions alone are not reliably preventing the generator from over-weighting those visual features.

### N19

N19 is useful for open-door / threshold direction, but it is outward-facing and strongly exposes bright exterior sky.

It is not the correct overall tonal target for N21.

### N20

N20 is the strongest approved continuity target for the next shot's overall visual world.

Its useful properties are:

- dim warm interior;
- no dominant direct exterior sunlight;
- warm sconces as readable practical sources;
- dark polished floor / stone;
- doorway presence without turning architecture into the hero;
- inward movement state.

Therefore:

`N20 = PRIMARY TONAL / LIGHTING CONTINUITY AUTHORITY FOR N21`

But the complete N20 Story Shot must not be delivered directly to generation because it contains Ning Qiushui / Jun Luyuan and complete wardrobe / body composition.

## 6. Candidate 07 camera strategy

The next image should not be a frontal architecture shot.

Preferred camera logic:

`INTERIOR-SIDE OBLIQUE / LOOKING DEEPER INWARD`

The doorway should be:

- large in implied scale;
- only partially visible;
- placed toward a side / rear edge of frame;
- not centered;
- not shown as a complete symmetrical arch;
- not treated as the hero object.

The camera should remain near normal human eye / chest height.

No low-angle monumental architecture presentation.

This allows the door to feel physically large without turning the shot into a cathedral establishing shot.
