## 2. Narrative function

N19 is the immediate visual answer to N18.

N18:
Jun Luyuan looks toward the clear daylight outside and doubts that rain is likely.

N19:
Ning Qiushui joins Jun's outward look and calmly explains that conditions here can change rapidly.

The image must read as:

`Jun is still considering the apparently good weather; Ning looks at the same exterior and answers from experience.`

No inward walking begins yet.

## 3. Locked composition

Preferred shot:

`MEDIUM TWO-SHOT / INTERIOR-SIDE ENTRANCE / SHARED OUTWARD GAZE`

- Ning and Jun remain inside / at the interior side of the castle entrance.
- Both orient toward the still-open doorway / bright exterior.
- The exterior remains calm and visibly fine.
- Ning is the speaking emphasis, but both characters remain clearly readable.
- Doorway and weather support the dialogue but do not overwhelm the two-character relationship.
- No camera-facing pose.

Preferred screen logic:

- Jun may remain closer to the doorway / exterior side of the pair.
- Ning stands immediately beside or slightly behind/inside Jun.
- Their gaze converges toward the same exterior direction.

## 4. Body Performance lock

### Ning Qiushui

Required performance:

- calm standing weight;
- torso and gaze turn toward the outside with Jun;
- restrained speaking state;
- one hand MAY lightly rest on the upper shoulder of Jun Luyuan.

Shoulder-contact interpretation:

`brief experienced-guide contact / casual grounding gesture`

It must NOT read as:

- an embrace;
- arm-around-shoulder intimacy;
- consoling;
- restraining;
- forceful pressure;
- staged friendship portrait.

The hand should rest naturally on the upper shoulder area with correct scale and relaxed fingers.

### Jun Luyuan

Required performance:

- continues looking outside;
- listens to Ning;
- no repeated N18 outward-presenting hand gesture;
- hands remain natural and secondary;
- no pocket pose;
- no model-like clothing adjustment.
