## 1. APPROVED CORRECTION

V0.1 framed N23 around:

`NEIL TURNS AND THE GROUP'S TRAVEL DIRECTION CHANGES`

Product Owner corrected the shot logic after checking continuity with the following A06 beat.

Approved V0.2 interpretation:

`NEIL REMAINS AT THE OPEN DOOR, WATCHING THE LAST PART OF THE GROUP ENTER THE CASTLE`

The group continues to move inward.

Neil has not yet left the doorway.

He does not close the door.

A06 / the following beat retains the visual continuation in which Neil leaves the open door behind and proceeds inward, while the open-door question is raised.

This is a deliberate continuity correction, not a rejection of the parent story event.

---

## 2. GOAL

Create a threshold-continuity shot that makes three facts readable at once:

1. the group is still entering / finishing entry;
2. Neil remains standing to one side of the open castle door;
3. Neil is not making any move to close the door.

The frame should quietly prepare the audience to notice:

`WHY IS THE DOOR STILL OPEN?`

without already staging the later spoken question.

---

## 3. SUCCESS

N23 succeeds if:

1. Neil is clearly identifiable at the doorway edge;
2. he stands aside rather than blocking the entrance;
3. he looks sideways toward the people entering;
4. the door remains visibly open;
5. several people continue moving into the castle in natural staggered positions;
6. the people read as the same cohort established in N22, seen from another real camera angle;
7. Guang Yong may continue his N22 backward-looking attention as a low-weight continuity cue;
8. Guang Yong does not become the primary subject;
9. the shot does not yet show Neil walking away from the door;
10. A06 still has room to own the next open-door / departure continuation.

---

## 4. EVIDENCE CLASS

### FACT

- Source story: after the group has entered, Neil does not close the door and takes the group toward the hall.
- N22 has already established the cohort as individual people inside the larger flow.
- Product Owner intentionally oriented Guang Yong backward in N22 to prepare his later question about the door.
- The following A06 beat concerns the still-open door and the question about why it is not closed.
- Same continuous castle entrance / interior-transition time and space applies.

### INFERENCE

- Holding Neil at the doorway for N23 creates a cleaner visual bridge into A06 than showing him already deep inside.
- Reusing the N22 cohort from a new angle strengthens continuity and avoids inventing a visually unrelated second crowd.
- Continuing Guang Yong's backward attention can make his later question feel motivated rather than abrupt.

### WORKING HYPOTHESIS

A single medium-wide threshold shot can support:
- Neil as doorway anchor,
- the open door as persistent fact,
- the cohort as continuing movement,
- Guang Yong as a subtle behavioral continuity cue,
without turning the frame into a staged ensemble.

### UNKNOWN

- Exact N23→A06 micro-cut timing.
- Exact number of visible people required for final generation.
- Exact degree of Guang Yong readability needed before he begins to compete with Neil.

## 6. CAMERA DESIGN

Target format:

`9:16 vertical`

Shot scale:

`MEDIUM-WIDE THRESHOLD GROUP SHOT`

Camera position:

- inside the castle;
- diagonally oriented toward the entrance;
- offset from the central doorway axis;
- positioned so Neil, the open door, and moving guests can coexist in frame;
- not a frontal architectural showcase.

Camera should feel like:

`ANOTHER REAL VIEWPOINT OF THE SAME N22 EVENT`

not:

`A MIRRORED OR FLIPPED COPY OF N22`

The relation between N22 and N23 is:

`SAME COHORT / SAME MOMENT CONTINUITY / DIFFERENT CAMERA POSITION`

---

## 7. NEIL BLOCKING

Neil is the principal continuity anchor.

Required state:

- standing at one side of the open doorway;
- upright and controlled;
- not touching the door;
- not reaching for the handle;
- not walking away yet;
- body broadly oriented toward the interior / threshold;
- head or eyes turned sideways toward the people passing into the castle;
- expression neutral, professional, restrained.

Neil must not:

- stare into camera;
- pose as a hero;
- make an inviting theatrical hand gesture;
- look sinister;
- close or partially close the door;
- already occupy a deep-interior position.

His unsettling quality comes from normal calm behavior while simply leaving the door open.

---

## 8. N22 CROWD CONTINUITY

N23 should visually reuse the cohort established in N22.

Continuity authority from N22:

- same overall clothing era / realism;
- same human proportion range;
- same mixed-group feeling;
- same warm-dark castle interior;
- same non-queue / non-lineup behavior;
- similar crowd density;
- natural overlap and staggered depth.

N23 must not reproduce the exact N22 composition.

The people can be shown through:

- backs;
- side-backs;
- partial profiles;
- cropped foreground bodies;
- overlapping midground figures.

Su Xiaoxiao and Liao Jian do not need to remain readable.

N23 is not a second character-seeding shot.

---

## 9. GUANG YONG CONTINUITY

Guang Yong may be retained as a low-weight continuity clue.

Approved behavioral continuity:

`N22 BACKWARD ATTENTION → N23 CONTINUED AWARENESS OF OPEN DOOR / NEIL → LATER A06 QUESTION`

In N23:

- Guang Yong may be one of the people already farther inside;
- he may glance back, side-back, or keep his head slightly oriented toward the doorway;
- he may be partially obscured;
- he does not need a clean frontal face;
- his pose must remain natural and restrained.

Hard hierarchy:

`NEIL + OPEN DOOR = PRIMARY STORY FACT`

`GROUP ENTRY = SECONDARY ACTION`

`GUANG YONG BACKWARD ATTENTION = TERTIARY CONTINUITY CUE`

Do not make Guang Yong:
- first-glance subject;
- large foreground portrait;
- obviously about to speak;
- theatrically pointing or staring.

---

## 10. GROUP BLOCKING

The cohort continues entering inward.

Preferred:

- 4–6 visible people or partial bodies;
- staggered depth;
- different stride phases;
- differing body angles;
- some already farther inside;
- some just clearing the threshold.

Avoid:

- queue;
- synchronized walking;
- all heads turned toward Neil;
- all backs perfectly aligned;
- cast lineup;
- ceremonial procession.

The group should feel caught during ordinary flow.

---

## 11. DOOR / SPACE FACT

The open castle door must remain clearly readable but should not become a standalone architecture shot.

Required:

- door open;
- threshold geometry continuous with Castle Entrance;
- both sides of the doorway remain part of the castle entrance/interior transition logic;
- exterior light remains controlled;
- no broad white daylight flooding the interior floor.

The viewer should notice the door because Neil is not closing it, not because the doorway is staged as a spectacle.

---

## 12. A06 CONTINUITY BOUNDARY

N23 ends before Neil has fully left the doorway.

A06 / following continuation owns:

- the open door remaining unattended;
- the audience / Guang Yong attention to that fact;
- Neil proceeding inward without going back to close it;
- the spoken open-door question.

Therefore N23 must not consume:

- Neil already walking toward the camera / deeper interior as the dominant action;
- Guang Yong clearly speaking;
- explicit question/reaction staging.

N23 is the visual setup.

A06 is the payoff / explicit question beat.
