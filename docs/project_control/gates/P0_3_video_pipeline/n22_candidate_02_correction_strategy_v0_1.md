## 6. COMPOSITION CORRECTION

Candidate 01 problem:

`CENTRAL SU + LIAO HERO PAIR / FOUR FACES READABLE`

Candidate 02 target:

`PASSING OBSERVATIONAL SLICE`

Hard composition rules:

- no named character on exact image centerline;
- Su and Liao must not share the same frontal focal plane;
- Su and Liao must not both be full-body dominant figures;
- at least one unnamed foreground occluder should break the frame;
- at least one retained named character should be partially occluded;
- no more than two named faces should be clearly readable at first glance;
- Guang Yong should require a second glance rather than first-glance recognition;
- no clean four-corner or triangular ensemble arrangement;
- no symmetric group spacing.

Preferred visual grammar:

`foreground obstruction → readable Su → offset Liao → partial Guang → deeper anonymous crowd`

This is a hierarchy, not a fixed pixel template.

## 7. LIGHTING CORRECTION

Candidate 01 failure:

- open doorway occupies too much bright area;
- strong cool-white exterior light dominates the right side;
- floor receives excessive bright reflection;
- low-key N20 continuity is weakened.

Candidate 02 lighting target:

`INTERIOR DOMINANT / EXTERIOR TRACE ONLY`

Hard rules:

1. Warm dim interior must occupy at least approximately `80–90%` of the perceived frame.
2. Exterior doorway should be:
   - mostly off-frame; or
   - reduced to a narrow edge / partial opening.
3. Exterior bright area should remain approximately `≤10–15%` of the image.
4. No direct white daylight beam across the floor.
5. No bright rim-light on all characters.
6. Floor should remain mostly dark / warm / low-reflectance.
7. Candle / wall-sconce practicals remain the primary visible light source.
8. Skin should remain natural, not orange.

Preferred camera relationship:

`CAMERA TURNED FURTHER INTO THE INTERIOR, AWAY FROM THE OPEN DOOR`

The open door remains a scene fact, not a compositional subject.
