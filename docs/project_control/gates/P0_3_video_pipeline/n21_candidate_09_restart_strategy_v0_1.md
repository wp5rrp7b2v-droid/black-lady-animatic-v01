Candidate 08 failed partly because the image organized itself around a clean central corridor / vanishing point.

`OFF-AXIS / INTERIOR-SIDE / OBLIQUE / HUMAN-EYE-HEIGHT`

Required:

- camera is laterally displaced from the main movement path;
- no central leader axis;
- no symmetrical doorway / arch framing;
- no complete grand double-door presentation;
- no centered vanishing point;
- architecture should be partially cropped / occluded rather than fully displayed.

`PARTIAL THRESHOLD AT ONE SIDE → IRREGULAR HUMAN CLUSTER → DARKER PARTLY HIDDEN INTERIOR`

The shot expands people scale, not building scale.

## 5. CROWD BLOCKING

Readable people:

`APPROXIMATELY 4–6`

Required arrangement:

- figures occupy different lateral positions;
- figures occupy different depths;
- at least one figure may be partially cropped by a frame edge;
- one or two figures may already be deeper inside;
- nearer figures must not line up behind a central leader;
- gait phases must differ;
- spacing must be visibly unequal;
- shoulder lines / body angles may vary slightly while preserving the same overall inward direction.

Preferred:

`STAGGERED / OVERLAPPING / ACCIDENTALLY OBSERVED`

Forbidden:

- front-two / middle-two / rear-one formal formation;
- single-file queue;
- V-shaped group;
- equal gaps;
- synchronized strides;
- clean team-entry composition;
- everyone fully readable.

Natural overlap is desirable.

---

## 6. ENVIRONMENT / UNKNOWN-SPACE READ

The deeper castle interior must not be fully explained.

Unknown-space feeling should come from:

- partial obstruction by people / columns / doorway edges;
- darker space beyond readable foreground;
- diminishing practical light;
- incomplete view of the route ahead;
- architecture leaving the frame before its full geometry is understood.

Do not show:

- a long clear corridor;
- a complete grand hall;
- repeated arches forming a clean perspective tunnel;
- highly symmetrical Gothic showcase architecture;
- large fully readable doorway geometry.

The viewer should understand:

`THEY ARE GOING IN`

but should not fully understand:

`WHAT THE SPACE AHEAD LOOKS LIKE`
