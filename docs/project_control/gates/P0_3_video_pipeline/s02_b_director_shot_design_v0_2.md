## 1. Revision reason

Product Owner directed the original `N19｜Conditions Can Change` dialogue beat to be split into two still Story Shots because the two clauses carry different visual actions:

1. `这里面瞬息万变，等你多来几次就知道了。`
2. `进去之后也记得不要乱碰东西。`

The first clause remains a response to Jun Luyuan's clear-weather doubt and stays at the entrance.
The second clause owns the physical transition away from the entrance and into the castle.

## 2. Locked revised sequence

`N16 → N17 → N18 → N19 → N20 → N21 → N22 → A06 → N23`

Mapping from V0.1:

- V0.1 N19 is split into new `N19` + new `N20`.
- V0.1 N20 `Sixteen Guests Enter` becomes `N21`.
- V0.1 N21 `Neil Leads Inward` becomes `N22`.
- `A06｜门不关` remains the same reused approved Story Shot.
- V0.1 N22 `Rain-Day Door Rule` becomes `N23`.

No story beat is deleted.
