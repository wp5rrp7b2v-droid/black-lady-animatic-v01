## 2. Director intent

S02-B is not primarily a Neil sequence.

Its dramatic progression is:

`Jun asks about his sister → Ning pulls him back to survival rules → clear weather creates false safety → Ning warns the Blood Door can change instantly → the full group enters → the still-open castle door becomes suspicious → Neil states the rain-day door rule and leads everyone onward`.

The visual center therefore shifts deliberately:

`Jun/Ning private dialogue → environment/weather → group scale → castle door → Neil`.

The design must not keep cutting back to Neil during the Jun/Ning exchange.

## 3. Locked source facts used by design

- S02-B begins immediately after the S02-A no-key reveal.
- Jun Luyuan asks Ning Qiushui whether his sister used to work in a place like this.
- Ning answers briefly and redirects Jun to the Blood Door task/rules.
- Survival reminders include not getting wet, not keeping the gaze fixed too long, and — by Product Owner auditory correction — not randomly touching things.
- Jun observes that the weather appears good / does not look like rain.
- Ning warns that conditions inside can change suddenly.
- Jun follows the group into the castle.
- Total visitors / ghost guests: 16; men and women are evenly split.
- Neil turns and leads the group toward the hall.
- An unidentified visitor asks why the castle door does not need to be closed.
- Neil states that the castle door closes only on rainy days and that this is the lady's requirement.
- Neil ends with “各位，请随我来”.
- The next narrative block starts with the hall / young-master description and belongs to the following sequence, not S02-B.

Story beat:
Jun asks about his sister; Ning gives the restrained “not entirely” response.

Composition:

A07 should remain available as a primary candidate for the opening of the next sequence.
