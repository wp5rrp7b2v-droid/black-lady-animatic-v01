## 4. Audience-retention design rules effective from N21

Every new visual node must add at least one new value:

- new information;
- new subject emphasis;
- new spatial information;
- new reaction;
- new risk cue;
- new question;
- new visual scale;
- new emotional or suspense pressure.

A node that merely shows the same people doing the same thing from another angle is not sufficient.

Preferred visual-function palette:

- `PRIMARY ACTION`
- `POV`
- `REACTION`
- `DETAIL`
- `GROUP SCALE`
- `ENVIRONMENT TRANSITION`
- `RISK PROMPT`
- `INFORMATION UPGRADE`

## 5. Ning Qiushui point-of-view rule

S02-B is not primarily a Neil sequence.

From N21 onward, visual organization should repeatedly return the viewer to:

`WHAT NING NOTICES / WHAT NING REGISTERS / WHAT NING UNDERSTANDS MAY MATTER`

This does NOT mean Ning must appear as the largest face in every shot.

Ning POV can be expressed through:

- over-shoulder composition;
- his line of sight;
- a reaction insert;
- an object / space detail that the edit implicitly attributes to his observation;
- a cut from Ning to what he notices.

The goal is to establish:

`NING = PRIMARY OBSERVATIONAL ANCHOR`

while Jun remains a secondary audience-entry character.

## 6. Jun Luyuan function

Jun should not disappear after N20.

His visual role from N21 onward is:

`AUDIENCE ENTRY / HUMAN-SCALE COMPANION`

Use Jun selectively when:

- the audience needs a readable non-expert response;
- a wider group shot risks becoming anonymous;
- Ning's experienced restraint benefits from contrast with Jun's more immediate attention.

Do NOT repeatedly return to a Ning-Jun two-shot merely because both characters are established.

V0.2 remaining structure:

| N26 | REACTION / PROTAGONIST ANCHOR | Ning registers “the lady's requirement” |

The composition sequence deliberately alternates:

`WIDE → POV/MID → SPATIAL WIDE → OTS/POV → DETAIL → MEDIUM → REACTION → SPATIAL ENDING`

This is designed to avoid repetitive medium two-shots / walking frames.

## 10. Color / look baseline effective from N21

S02-B new images must reduce later mastering burden.

Baseline:

### Color temperature

- interior practical lighting: warm amber / tungsten family;
- skin remains natural, not orange;
- exterior daylight, where visible, may be cooler / more neutral than interior;
- do not alternate arbitrarily between orange-heavy and neutral-gray renders.

### Exposure

- low-key interior;
- faces / important silhouettes remain readable;
- doorway may be brighter but must not become a blown white portal;
- target transition: doorway brighter than interior by roughly a controlled 1–1.5-stop visual impression, not an extreme HDR split.

### Black level

- deep blacks are allowed;
- retain texture in dark clothing, stone and hair;
- avoid crushed featureless black patches.

### Saturation

- restrained / low-to-moderate;
- warm practical lights may carry color;
- clothing and skin should not become vividly saturated.

### Contrast

- moderate cinematic contrast;
- no flat gray image;
- no aggressive high-contrast “poster” rendering.

### Cross-shot rule

When two adjacent shots share the same space and time:

`MATCH LIGHT DIRECTION + MATCH WARMTH FAMILY + MATCH BLACK FLOOR + MATCH SATURATION RANGE`

Exact final matching remains a later:

`CHAPTER MASTERING PASS`

but gross variation must be prevented at generation stage.

## 12. Direct answers to the six required design questions

### Q1. N21 onward where should new visual nodes be added?

Add:

- N22 inside the old N21 parent beat;
- N24 inside the old N22 parent beat;
- N26 and N27 inside the old N23 parent beat.

Net result:

old 4 visual beats → new 8 visual beats including A06.

### Q2. Which places cannot remain one long still?

Must split:

- old N21 / 9.6s group-entry + sixteen-person narration;
- old N22 / 7.34s Neil-leading movement;
- old N23 / 7.76s rain-rule answer + lady requirement + “follow me”.

They contain multiple semantic functions and should not be visually compressed into one static composition.

### Q3. How does Ning's POV continue?

Through:

- N22 observational cohort POV;
- N24 spatial following POV;
- N26 rule-reaction anchor.

Ning does not need to dominate every image, but the edit repeatedly returns to what he is noticing.

### Q4. Where should Jun appear?

Jun remains useful:

- N21 as recognizable continuity inside the cohort;
- optionally N24 near Ning as human-scale companion;
- optionally soft-secondary in N26.

He should not receive another independent hero shot unless later audio gives him a new narrative function.

### Q5. Which detail / reaction shots break repetition?

- A06 = door detail / risk prompt;
- N22 = observational people-in-group POV;
- N26 = Ning reaction to the lady's rule.

These break the walking-wide / speaking-medium repetition.

### Q6. How does S02-B end with stronger suspense?

N26 makes the rain-door rule land through Ning's attention.

N27 then moves the cohort across a darker interior threshold rather than ending on Neil's speaking face.

The sequence therefore ends on:

`RULE MYSTERY + FORWARD MOVEMENT INTO UNKNOWN SPACE`

rather than simple exposition.

## 13. N21 named-character seeding compatibility

Therefore the previous N21 single-shot concept is intentionally decompressed into two functional images.
