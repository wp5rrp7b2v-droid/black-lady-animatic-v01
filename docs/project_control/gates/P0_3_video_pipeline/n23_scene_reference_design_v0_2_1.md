---

## 2. STORY MOMENT — TAIL END OF ENTRY

N23 occurs when the group is already almost fully inside the castle.

Therefore the visual state is not:

`NEIL WATCHING PEOPLE BEGIN TO ENTER FROM THE DOOR`

It is:

`NEIL WATCHING THE LAST PART / TAIL OF THE GROUP AS ALMOST EVERYONE HAS ALREADY MOVED INTO THE CASTLE`

This distinction controls Neil's gaze.

---

## 3. NEIL GAZE — CORRECTED HARD LOCK

Neil remains beside the open door and is approximately near-frontal to camera.

However:

`NEIL'S HEAD / EYES TURN SLIGHTLY TO FRAME-RIGHT`

His attention is on the people who have already moved into the castle and are now to his right.

He is NOT primarily watching:

- the open door itself;
- the exterior;
- frame-left;
- another incoming group.

Correct read:

`NEIL IS CHECKING / WATCHING THE GROUP TAIL AS THEY FINISH MOVING INTO THE CASTLE`

This remains restrained and observational, not suspicious or theatrical.

---

## 4. CROWD STATE

The crowd still moves:

`LEFT → RIGHT`

But N23 is at the late stage of that movement.

Preferred staging:

- most visible people are already right of Neil;
- only the last one or two people / partial bodies may still be near the left-side doorway;
- the visual mass should sit more toward middle-right / right;
- the doorway is visibly open behind / left of Neil;
- the group should feel almost completely inside.

This supports the later question:

`WHY IS THE DOOR STILL OPEN IF EVERYONE IS ALREADY INSIDE?`

---

## 5. SCREEN LAYOUT

### Left
- open door;

### Center / left-center
- Neil beside the door;
- near-frontal;
- restrained;
- head / eyes subtly to the RIGHT.

### Middle-right / right
- the majority of the cohort already inside;
- left→right travel continuation;
- darker interior;
- crowd tail moving away from threshold.

## 6. NARRATIVE EFFECT

The shot should communicate:

1. the group is almost fully inside;
2. Neil remains at the open door;
3. Neil watches the people to his right rather than attending to the door;
4. he still makes no move to close it.

This strengthens the setup for A06 because the open door becomes increasingly conspicuous after the group has nearly finished entering.

- door / exterior light remain LEFT;
- group remains LEFT → RIGHT;

- Neil remains near-frontal beside door;

- Neil closes or touches the door;
- crowd approaches camera as an ensemble;
- N22-like frontal observer composition returns.
