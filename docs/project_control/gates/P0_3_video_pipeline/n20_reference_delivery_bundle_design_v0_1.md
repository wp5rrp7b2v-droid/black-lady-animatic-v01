## 2. Spatial geometry interpretation

The approved N20 Scene Reference Design contains two simultaneous requirements:

1. rear or rear-3Q walking/following view;
2. open doorway / exterior daylight remains readable as the space they are leaving behind.

A strict centered rear-following camera would place the entrance largely behind the camera and make the doorway itself difficult or impossible to read.

Therefore Bundle V0.1 interprets the approved design as:

`INTERIOR-SIDE REAR-3Q OBLIQUE FOLLOWING VIEW`

Camera:

- located inside the castle;
- offset laterally from the characters' walking axis;
- behind and to one side of the pair rather than directly centered behind them;
- sees both characters moving diagonally deeper into the interior;
- preserves a readable slice / opening / spill of bright exterior light at the side-rear portion of frame;
- does not turn around to face the characters.

This is an execution clarification inside the approved `REAR OR REAR-3Q` range, not a new Director Design.

## 5. Face-visibility rule

N20 is movement-first, not portrait-first.

Hard rule:

`Do not rotate either character toward camera merely to show the face.`

Allowed:

- rear;
- rear 3/4;
- partial profile from a natural slight speaking turn;
- limited facial visibility.

If facial visibility conflicts with believable walking direction:

`walking direction and body mechanics win.`

This rule directly prevents the failure mode seen in earlier S02-B attempts where characters were unnaturally turned toward camera.
