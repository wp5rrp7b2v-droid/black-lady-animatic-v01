# SCENE_CASTLE_ENTRANCE_INNER_LOBBY｜Scene Asset Design V0.1

Date:

`2026-10-06`

Status:

`PRODUCT OWNER APPROVED / LOCKED`

Asset class:

`SCENE MASTER`

Production route:

`COMMON VISUAL ASSET / DIRECT WORK GENERATION / NO STORY SHOT BUNDLE`

## Purpose

Create the reusable interior lobby immediately inside the castle main entrance.

Camera position:

`AT / NEAR THE CASTLE MAIN DOOR THRESHOLD, LOOKING INWARD INTO THE CASTLE`

This asset is not a reverse shot toward the door.

It establishes the space visible after crossing the main door and before reaching the First Hall.

## Capacity

The space must comfortably support:

`AT LEAST 5–8 PEOPLE NATURALLY DISPERSED`

Requirements:

- natural stagger / depth;
- enough room to stop and wait;
- no forced queue;
- not cramped;
- not so large that it reads as a formal main hall.

The full narrative group may contain 16 people; this Scene Master must at minimum support 5–8 people within a single usable shot composition.

## Spatial identity

- same Castle Entrance spatial system as SCENE_CASTLE_ENTRANCE V002;
- immediately inside the main entrance;
- transitional lobby / holding area;
- before First Hall;
- not a separate grand room.

## Visual requirements

- 4:3 horizontal;
- 1536 × 1152 preferred;
- empty environment;
- warm low-key interior;
- restrained amber / tungsten practical light;
- weak daylight cue from entrance direction;
- aged stone / plaster;
- dark wood trim;
- restrained Gothic detail;
- open central / midground standing area;
- clear continuation deeper into castle;
- clean and reusable Scene Master.

## Door treatment

The door is not the subject.

Allowed:

- tiny edge of frame / door leaf;
- partial jamb;
- entrance light direction.

Preferred:

`THE VIEW EMPHASIZES THE INNER LOBBY, NOT THE DOOR ITSELF`

## Hard exclusions

- people;
- First Hall;
- fireplace;
- dining-hall cues;
- long table;
- giant staircase;
- palace / hotel-lobby scale;
- large decorative furniture or floral arrangement as focal point;
- church-like composition;
- long corridor;
- dominant exterior view;
- dominant door;
- strong daylight flooding deep inside;
- bright studio exposure;
- text / signage;
- dirty noise / cutout appearance.

## PASS definition

The first read should be:

`I AM STANDING AT THE CASTLE ENTRANCE AND LOOKING INTO A COMPACT INNER LOBBY WHERE 5–8 PEOPLE COULD ENTER, PAUSE, WAIT AND TURN BEFORE MOVING DEEPER INTO THE CASTLE. THIS IS NOT YET THE FIRST HALL.`
